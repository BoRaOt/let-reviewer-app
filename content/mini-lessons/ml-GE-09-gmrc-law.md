The **GMRC and Values Education Act (RA 11476, 2020)** puts ethics formally into the K to 12 curriculum.

**Good Manners and Right Conduct (GMRC)** means universally accepted basic social values and etiquette, proper behavior that conveys respect to the people one deals with. **Values Education** covers universal human, ethical and moral values, such as respect for oneself, others and the environment.

The law makes both **replace Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao**:
- **GMRC** is taught from **Grades 1 to 6** as a **separate subject**, with the same time allotment as other core subjects, and is integrated into daily activities in **kindergarten**.
- **Values Education** is taught from **Grades 7 to 10** as a separate subject with the same time allotment, and GMRC stays integrated within it.

DepEd must use concrete character-building activities, such as role-playing and community immersion, rather than lectures alone.
Memory aid: "GMRC 1–6, Values Ed 7–10, both replace EsP."
