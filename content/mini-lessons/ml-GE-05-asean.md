The **Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN)** was created on **8 August 1967**, when the foreign ministers of **five** countries, **Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand**, signed the **ASEAN Declaration** in **Bangkok**. Its forerunner was the Association of Southeast Asia (1961) of Thailand, the Philippines and Malaya. ASEAN began partly to contain communism, then shifted toward economic development and regional stability.

It grew in stages: **Brunei in 1984**, **Vietnam in 1995**, **Laos and Myanmar in 1997**, and **Cambodia in 1999**. Members signed a binding **ASEAN Charter in 2008**. Wikipedia reports that **Timor-Leste** joined in October 2025 as the eleventh member; confirm with an official ASEAN source.

The first ASEAN summit, in Bali in 1976, produced the Treaty of Amity and Cooperation. The Philippines, as a founding member, works through ASEAN on trade, security and South China Sea issues.
