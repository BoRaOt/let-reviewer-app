# HE-A3 — Introduction to ICT

Annex F gives this exploratory course **10 items** (Remember 1, Understand 2, Apply 5, Analyze 1, Evaluate 1). Outcomes: computing, science and math knowledge; **hardware and software components**; **responsible digital citizenship** (data privacy, online safety, ethics); **productivity software**; uses and **categories of application software**. [SRC-0002, Annex F, A.3]

## 1. Plain explanation

**A computer** is a programmable machine that follows stored instructions. Every computer works through the **information processing cycle: input → processing → output → storage**.

**Hardware** is the physical part: the motherboard, the CPU (the "brain"), memory and storage. **RAM** is temporary working memory that clears when power is off. **ROM** holds the start-up instructions. Hard disks, flash drives and cloud storage are **secondary (permanent) storage**.

**Software** has two big families. **System software** runs the computer (the operating system, drivers, antivirus). **Application software** does the user's tasks: word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, databases, browsers, and specialized programs.

**Networks** connect two or more devices: PAN (personal), LAN (local), WAN (wide). Routers direct traffic between networks.

**Digital citizenship** means using technology safely, securely, ethically and legally. In the Philippines that includes the **Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)**, the **Cybercrime Prevention Act (RA 10175)** and the **Intellectual Property Code (RA 8293)**.

## 2. Testable facts

**Hardware and processing (OpenStax Workplace Software and Skills 1.2)**
- Computers store programs and data as strings of **1s and 0s**. One binary digit is a **bit**; **8 bits make a byte**; a megabyte is a little over a million bytes. [SRC-0103, 5.1]
- A computer is a **programmable machine** that executes predefined lists of instructions and responds to new instructions. [SRC-0102, 1.2]
- The **motherboard** runs communications for the system and sits in the **system unit**; the **CPU** contains the **microprocessor**, the "brains", with a control unit and an **arithmetic-logic unit**. [SRC-0102, 1.2 "How Computers Work"]
- Information processing cycle: **input, processing, output, storage**. [SRC-0102, 1.2 "Information Processing"]
- **Primary storage = RAM**: temporary, short-term, available only while the computer is on; more RAM means programs run faster. **Secondary storage** (hard disks, flash drives, CDs) is permanent. [SRC-0102, 1.2]
- **ROM** holds hardwired instructions the computer needs **before the operating system loads**. [SRC-0102, 1.2]
- The **operating system** controls memory and processes, lets hardware and software communicate, and gives the user an interface (e.g. Windows, macOS, Android). [SRC-0102, 1.2 "Operating Systems"]
- Networks: **PAN** (devices in a small area), **LAN** (e.g. a campus linking computers and printers), **WAN** (large regions; the internet is the largest). A **router** directs traffic between networks and lets several computers share one internet connection. [SRC-0102, 1.2 "Networks"]
- A **client** requests resources on a network; a **server** connects devices and shares resources. [SRC-0102, 1.2 "Network Servers"]

**Software (2.1)**
- Two major categories: **system software** (operating system, printer and keyboard drivers, **antivirus**) and **application software** (task-oriented programs). **Web browsers** are application programs. [SRC-0102, 2.1]
- Program interface parts: **GUI**; **menu bar / ribbon** (general commands, top); **toolbar** (icons for common commands); **dialog box** (gives information or asks for input); **status bar** (bottom; e.g. page count). [SRC-0102, 2.1]
- Productivity applications: **word processing** (text documents: memos, letters, reports), **spreadsheet** (organize and analyze numeric data, charts), **presentation** (slideshows of text and graphics), **database** (organize and store large amounts of data). [SRC-0102, 2.1 "Common Applications"]
- **Specialized applications** serve particular occupations (e.g. accounting software); **mobile applications** are designed for phones and tablets. [SRC-0102, 2.1]

**Online safety and ethics (1.4)**
- **Computer security** protects systems and information from unauthorized use; **computer ethics** are guidelines for morally acceptable use; **cybercrime** is any criminal offense involving a computer and a network. [SRC-0102, 1.4]
- Protective strategies: security suites, a **firewall** (barrier between a secured and an unsecured network), password-protected network access, avoiding logins on **open networks**, and **encryption**. [SRC-0102, 1.4]
- **Phishing** lures users to fake emails or sites to give personal information; it usually relies on **spoofing** (messages that look as if they come from a trusted source). [SRC-0102, 1.4 "Data and Identity Theft"]
- Strong passwords: at least **eight characters**, mix letters and numbers, upper and lower case and symbols; no real names or whole words; **do not reuse**. **Biometrics** (fingerprints, retinal scans) are harder to steal. [SRC-0102, 1.4 "Password Management and Biometrics"]
- **Cookies** are small data files websites leave on your device to track browsing and search history. [SRC-0102, 1.4 "Cookies"]

**Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**
- **Personal information:** information from which a person's identity is apparent or can be reasonably and directly ascertained, or that identifies them when combined with other information. [SRC-0045, Sec. 3(g)]
- **Sensitive personal information** includes race, ethnic origin, marital status, age, religion, political affiliation; **health, education**, genetic or sexual life; government-issued numbers such as **SSS numbers, health records, licenses, tax returns**. [SRC-0045, Sec. 3(l)]
- **Consent** must be freely given, specific and informed, evidenced by written, electronic or recorded means. [SRC-0045, Sec. 3(b)]
- Processing follows the principles of **transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality**. [SRC-0045, Sec. 11]
- Rights of the data subject include: to be **informed**; **reasonable access**; to **dispute** and have errors **corrected**; to have data **blocked, removed or destroyed** when incomplete, outdated, false or unlawfully obtained; and to be **indemnified** for damages. [SRC-0045, Sec. 16]
- The law creates the **National Privacy Commission** as an independent body to administer it. [SRC-0045, Sec. 7]

**Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (RA 10175)**
- Offenses against confidentiality, integrity and availability: **illegal access**, **illegal interception**, **data interference** and **system interference** (both include introducing **viruses**), **misuse of devices**, **cyber-squatting** (bad-faith domain names). [SRC-0046, Sec. 4(a)]
- Computer-related offenses: **forgery, fraud, identity theft**. Content-related offenses: **cybersex, child pornography, unsolicited commercial communications, libel** (Revised Penal Code Art. 355 libel committed through a computer system). [SRC-0046, Sec. 4(b)–(c)]
- Crimes under the Revised Penal Code and special laws committed **through ICT** carry a penalty **one degree higher**. [SRC-0046, Sec. 6]

**Intellectual property (RA 8293)**
- **Computer programs** are protected literary and artistic works, protected **from the moment of creation**. [SRC-0044, Sec. 172.1(n)]
- The lawful owner of a program may make **one back-up copy** or adaptation without the copyright owner's permission, when needed for use or archival purposes. [SRC-0044, Sec. 189.1]
- Copyright generally lasts for the **author's life plus 50 years**. [SRC-0044, Sec. 213.1]

## 3. Common mistakes

- **RAM vs ROM.** RAM is temporary working memory (lost at power-off). ROM holds start-up instructions. Neither is "the hard disk".
- **Antivirus is system software**, not an application; a **browser is an application**.
- **Storage is the last step** of the IPOS cycle, after output.
- **Age, education and health are sensitive personal information** under RA 10173, not ordinary information.
- **Online libel** is punished under RA 10175 (via RPC Art. 355), one degree higher than ordinary libel.
- **Phishing vs spoofing.** Spoofing is the disguise (a fake sender); phishing is the lure to steal data.
- **Registration is not needed** for copyright; it exists from creation.

## 4. Memory aid

**"I Process Output Saved"** = Input, Processing, Output, Storage.
**Data subject rights "I-A-C-E-D":** Informed, Access, Correct, Erase/block, Damages.
