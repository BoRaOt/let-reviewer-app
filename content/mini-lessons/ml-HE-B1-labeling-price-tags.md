The Consumer Act sets minimum label contents for consumer products sold in the Philippines, whether local or imported:
- the registered trade or brand name;
- the registered trademark;
- the registered business name;
- the Philippine address of the manufacturer, importer or repacker;
- the general make or active ingredients;
- the net quantity of contents in metric units;
- the country of manufacture if imported.

The metric system is the official system for weights and measures in products and commercial transactions. Retail goods must carry a price tag, label or marking displayed to the public. A seller may not charge more than the tagged price, and must charge the same price to every buyer. In a classroom scenario where a store charges more at the counter than the shelf tag shows, the violated rule is the price tag requirement.
Memory aid: "Name, mark, maker, makeup, measure, made-in — and the price you see is the price you pay."
Sources: SRC-0068 Arts. 63, 77, 81
