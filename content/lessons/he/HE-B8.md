# HE-B8 — Appropriate tools, equipment, materials and technology in HE

Annex F gives this line **4 items** (Understand 2, Apply 2). [SRC-0002, Annex F, B.8] Expect "which tool or material fits this task" and "what to do with a faulty tool" questions.

The TESDA training regulations for HE trades list the tools and materials each trade uses. This lesson collects the ones most likely to be tested: kitchen cleaning, baking, sewing and housekeeping. General tool maintenance (segregate and label defective tools) is in HE-A5.

## 1. Plain explanation

**Match the tool to the job and the material.** Cleaning agents are chosen for the surface (glass, wood, steel, carpet). Baking equipment is chosen for the product (mixers, scales, pans, moulds). Sewing tools are chosen for the step (measure, cut, sew, press).

**Keep tools clean and working.** Kitchen equipment is cleaned and sanitized with potable water and the right chemicals, then stored safely. Sewing machines are cleaned and lubricated as the manufacturer says. Faults are reported, not hidden.

**Measure accurately.** Bakers **weigh** and measure ingredients by recipe; dressmakers use tape measures and rulers made for curves and right angles.

**Use protective clothing** when handling cleaning chemicals.

## 2. Testable facts

**Kitchen cleaning (TESDA Cookery NC II, "Clean and maintain kitchen premises")**
- Elements: (1) clean, sanitize and store equipment; (2) clean and sanitize premises; (3) dispose of waste. [SRC-0075, clean and maintain kitchen premises unit]
- Equipment and utensils are cleaned and sanitized with **chemicals and clean potable water**, following **manufacturer's instructions**, then stored or stacked safely in the designated place. [SRC-0075, kitchen premises unit, element 1]
- Premises are cleaned on a **cleaning schedule**; walls, floors, shelves and working surfaces are cleaned without damage to health or property; **first aid** procedures are followed if an accident happens. [SRC-0075, kitchen premises unit, element 2]
- Wastes are **sorted** and disposed of per sanitary regulations; cleaning **chemicals are disposed of safely**. [SRC-0075, kitchen premises unit, element 3]
- Cleaning equipment and supplies listed: **chemical dispensers**; paper towels, cleaning agents and **sanitizers**. [SRC-0075, kitchen premises unit, range of variables 3]
- Required knowledge includes **environment-friendly** cleaning products and practices, and **cross-contamination** issues. [SRC-0075, kitchen premises unit, evidence guide 2]

**Baking (TESDA Bread and Pastry Production NC II, "Prepare and produce bakery products")**
- Ingredients are **selected, measured and weighed** according to the recipe; the **oven temperature** is chosen for the desired product characteristics. [SRC-0076, bakery products unit, elements 1.1, 1.5]
- Baking equipment: commercial **mixers and attachments**, cutting implements, **scales**, measures, bowls, **ovens**, moulds, shapes and cutters, baking sheets and pans. [SRC-0076, bakery products unit, range of variables 4]
- Bread-baking techniques: beating, whisking, **folding**, rolling, **laminating**, **creaming**, **kneading**, incorporating fat, make-up and **proofing**; plus scaling, panning and baking. [SRC-0076, bakery products unit, range of variables 5]
- Bakery product types: **lean-yeast dough** (crisp- and soft-crusted breads, rye/whole wheat, sourdough), **soft dough** (loaves and buns), **rich-yeast dough** (sweet dough, rolled-in/laminated dough), **quick breads** (muffins, shortcakes), doughnuts and pancakes, cookies. [SRC-0076, bakery products unit, range of variables 2]
- Product quality is judged by **color, consistency and texture, moisture, mouth feel, appearance**. [SRC-0076, bakery products unit, range of variables 3]
- Storage considers **temperature, light and air exposure**, proper containers, **labeling**, display cabinets and refrigeration or freezing time. [SRC-0076, bakery products unit, range of variables 9]

**Sewing (TESDA Dressmaking NC II)**
- Machine types: **single-needle lockstitch**, double-needle lockstitch, **edging** machine, **buttonhole** machine. [SRC-0080, "Set up and operate machine", range of variables 1]
- Basic machine maintenance: operate and monitor the machine; **clean and lubricate** it per the manufacturer's instructions; check operation and **document and report** problems to the supervisor. [SRC-0080, "Perform basic maintenance", elements 1–3]
- **Minor** machine faults: loose threads, broken needle, machine control. **Major** faults: electric motor defect, **timing not synchronized**, broken body parts. [SRC-0080, "Perform basic maintenance", range of variables]
- Measuring tools for pattern drafting: **tape measure, hip curve rule, L-square, meter stick**. [SRC-0080, "Draft and cut pattern", range of variables 4]

**Housekeeping (TESDA Housekeeping NC II, "Clean public areas, facilities and equipment")**
- Select equipment for the type of cleaning; check it is clean and safe **before use**; choose **dry and wet cleaning agents** per the manufacturer and OSH rules; wear **protective clothing** where needed. [SRC-0078, clean public areas unit, element 1]
- Cleaning equipment and chemicals are chosen according to the **type of material** being cleaned: glass, leather, fabrics, brass/steel, wood, carpet, hard floors. [SRC-0078, clean public areas unit, element 2 and range of variables 3]
- Protective clothing: overalls, jackets, aprons, **goggles and masks**, waterproof clothing and footwear, headwear. [SRC-0078, clean public areas unit, range of variables 2]
- Hazards to watch for include spills, **wet or slippery surfaces**, fumes, blood, **needles and syringes**, broken glass, sharp objects, and hot utensils and surfaces. [SRC-0078, clean public areas unit, range of variables 5]

## 3. Common mistakes

- **Weigh, don't guess** in baking. The TR says ingredients are *measured and weighed*.
- **Lean vs rich dough.** Lean-yeast dough makes crusty breads and sourdough; rich-yeast dough (with more fat and sugar) makes sweet and laminated doughs. Muffins are *quick* breads (no yeast).
- **Minor vs major sewing-machine faults.** A broken needle is minor; a motor defect or out-of-time hook is major and goes to a technician.
- **Same chemical for every surface** is wrong; choose by material.
- **Cleaning chemicals are not poured anywhere**; they are disposed of by standard procedure.
