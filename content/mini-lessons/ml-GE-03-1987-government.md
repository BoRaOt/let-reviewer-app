The **1987 Constitution** divides power among three branches. **Legislative power** belongs to **Congress**, made up of the **Senate** and the **House of Representatives** (Article VI). **Executive power** belongs to the **President** (Article VII). **Judicial power** belongs to **one Supreme Court** and the lower courts (Article VIII). The rights of every person against government abuse are in the **Bill of Rights, Article III**.

Term limits are a favorite exam item. **Senators** serve six years and no more than **two consecutive terms**. **House members** serve three years and no more than **three consecutive terms**. The **President** serves **six years** and is **not eligible for any re-election**.

Local government: the political subdivisions are **provinces, cities, municipalities and barangays**, plus autonomous regions in Muslim Mindanao and the Cordilleras. A region in general is not a local government unit.
Memory aid: "Senate 6×2, House 3×3, President 6 once."
