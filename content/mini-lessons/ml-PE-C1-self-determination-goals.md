**Intrinsic motivation** comes from within, such as enjoying sewing for its own sake. **Extrinsic motivation** comes from outside, such as grades or prizes. **Self-determination theory** says these are not opposites but a **continuum**, and motivation grows when three needs are met: **autonomy** (feeling free to choose), **competence** (feeling capable) and **relatedness** (feeling connected to others). Teachers support competence with tasks that are challenging but achievable.

Goals matter too. **Mastery goals**, aiming to learn and improve, sustain interest better than **performance goals**, aiming to look better than others, which can breed unhealthy competition.

Carole **Ames's TARGET** model gathers these ideas into six areas a teacher controls: **Task**, **Authority** (sharing choices), **Recognition**, **Grouping**, **Evaluation** and **Time**.
Memory aid: "Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness; aim for mastery; TARGET = Task, Authority, Recognition, Grouping, Evaluation, Time."
