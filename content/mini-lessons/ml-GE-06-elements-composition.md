To describe an artwork, start with its **elements**, the vocabulary artists use.
- **Line:** the path the eye follows.
- **Shape:** a flat, two-dimensional area, geometric or organic.
- **Form:** a three-dimensional object with length, width and depth, as in sculpture.
- **Color:** hues with their value and intensity.
- **Value:** lightness and darkness; strong differences create contrast.
- **Texture:** surface quality, either real and touchable or implied in a painting.
- **Space:** positive space, filled by objects, and negative space around them.

Then look at **composition**, which means "putting together": how the elements are organized. It is different from the subject. Common techniques are the **rule of thirds**, placing key parts along lines dividing the frame into thirds; the **rule of odds**; leaving space where a figure looks; simplifying; using symmetry; and guiding the eye to create movement.
