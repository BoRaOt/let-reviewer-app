The 1987 Constitution sets the direction: "The State shall pursue an **independent foreign policy**," with **national sovereignty, territorial integrity, national interest and the right to self-determination** as the paramount considerations. It also adopts a policy of **freedom from nuclear weapons** in Philippine territory.

**Overseas Filipino Workers** link the country to the world economy. Their remittances reached about **₱1.9 trillion in 2022**, roughly **8.9 percent of GDP**. The term spread with **RA 8042, the Migrant Workers and Overseas Filipinos Act of 1995**.

The **West Philippine Sea** is the Philippine name for the parts of the South China Sea inside the country's **exclusive economic zone**, required in government use by **Administrative Order No. 29 (2012)**. On **12 July 2016**, the **Permanent Court of Arbitration** ruled in favor of the Philippines in its case on the South China Sea.
