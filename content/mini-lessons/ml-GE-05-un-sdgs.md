The **United Nations** was established by the **UN Charter**, signed on **26 June 1945** in San Francisco, and came into existence on **24 October 1945**, now celebrated as UN Day. Its main aims are international peace and security and friendly relations among nations. Its headquarters is in **New York**, and it has **193 member states**.

The UN has **six principal organs**: the **General Assembly**, where all members meet; the **Security Council**, responsible for peace and security; the **Economic and Social Council**; the **International Court of Justice** in The Hague; the **Secretariat**, headed by the Secretary-General; and the **Trusteeship Council**.

In **2015**, all UN members adopted the **17 Sustainable Development Goals** as part of the **2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development**, covering poverty, hunger, health, education, gender equality, clean water, climate action and more. Progress reports show many targets are off track.
