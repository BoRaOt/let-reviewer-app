The **Masustansyang Pagkain para sa Batang Pilipino Act (RA 11037, 2018)** set up a national feeding program against undernutrition.

**DepEd** runs the **school-based feeding program** for undernourished public school children from **kindergarten to Grade 6**. **DSWD** runs supplemental feeding in day care centers for children aged **3 to 5**. Both must give **at least one fortified meal** a day for **at least 120 days** a year.

A **fortified meal** has added micronutrients and supplies at least **one-third of the daily requirement** under the Philippine Dietary Reference Intakes. Meals follow **cycle menus** set with the National Nutrition Council and FNRI and adapted to age, place and local or religious food preferences.

The program also includes fresh milk, micronutrient supplements and iodized salt, deworming and health checks, **Gulayan sa Paaralan** school gardens, water and sanitation facilities, and nutrition education.
