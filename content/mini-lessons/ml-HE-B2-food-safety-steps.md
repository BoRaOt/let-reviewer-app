Safe food handling rests on four steps.
- **Clean:** wash hands well, clean surfaces often, wash utensils after each use, and rinse fruits and vegetables even if you will peel them.
- **Separate:** prevent cross-contamination by using different cutting boards for produce and for meat, poultry, seafood and eggs, and by storing them apart.
- **Cook:** heat food to proper temperatures and check with a food thermometer.
- **Chill:** refrigerate leftovers within two hours, and never thaw or marinate food on the counter.

Our source keeps hot food above 140 degrees Fahrenheit and cold food at 40 degrees Fahrenheit, or 4 degrees Celsius, or lower. Store raw meat on the lowest shelf so its juices cannot drip onto other foods.
