TESDA trains assessors to run a competency assessment in six steps.
1. **Organize** the assessment: identify activities using the Evidence Guide, prepare resources in a safe setting, and inform the right people.
2. **Prepare the candidate:** explain the purpose, the competency standard, the evidence to be collected and the procedure, and check whether the candidate needs an allowable adjustment.
3. **Gather evidence** using the methods in the Evidence Guide, such as demonstration with oral questioning, written tests or portfolios, and document it with the assessment tools.
4. **Make the decision:** judge the evidence against the rules of evidence and the dimensions of competency. The result is competent or not yet competent.
5. **Record** results accurately and keep them confidential.
6. **Give feedback:** be clear and constructive, explore gaps, and tell the candidate about reassessment.

An HE teacher uses the same steps for a cookery practical.
Memory aid: "Only Prepared Grads Do Real Fieldwork" = Organize, Prepare, Gather, Decide, Record, Feedback.
