Philippine law gives small businesses several shapes and sizes.
- **MSMEs:** under the Magna Carta for MSMEs, they may be single proprietorships, cooperatives, partnerships or corporations, sized by total assets excluding land. Micro means not more than three million pesos; small means above three million up to fifteen million.
- **BMBEs:** a barangay micro business enterprise, with assets of not more than three million pesos, can receive special incentives under Republic Act 9178.
- **Cooperatives:** an autonomous, registered association of people with a common bond of interest. Members join voluntarily, contribute capital fairly, use its services, and share its risks and benefits. Membership is voluntary and open, and members control it democratically, with one member one vote in primary cooperatives.
- **Corporations:** under the Revised Corporation Code, a corporation may have up to fifteen incorporators, a single person may form a One Person Corporation, and corporations exist perpetually unless their articles say otherwise.
Memory aid: "Co-op = one member, one vote."
Sources: SRC-0064 Sec. 3; SRC-0065 Sec. 3; SRC-0066 Arts. 3–4; SRC-0067 Secs. 10–11
