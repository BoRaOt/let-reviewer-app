**Gregor Mendel** (1822–1884) was an Augustinian friar in Brno, in today's Czech Republic. Breeding **garden peas**, he showed that traits pass from parents to offspring **independently** of other traits and in **dominant and recessive** patterns. A dominant trait appears unchanged in hybrids; a recessive trait shows only when no dominant form is present. He published his work in 1865.

Living things are named and grouped with the **Linnaean** system, after Swedish scientist Carl **Linnaeus**. The groups become more specific step by step. Today's full classification has **eight levels**: **domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus and species**. The dog is Eukarya, Animalia, Chordata, Mammalia, Carnivora, Canidae, *Canis lupus*. Cats share the dog's first five levels.

Older reviewers start at kingdom and list seven levels. If the exam lists eight, domain comes first.
