The Code on Sanitation (PD 856) sets the basic rules for any place where food or drinks are made, processed, stored, sold or served, including a school canteen.

The **establishment** needs a **sanitary permit** from the local health office, posted where customers can see it. If the owner or occupant changes, the change must be noted within fourteen days.

Each **worker** needs a **health certificate** from the local health authority, given only after a physical and medical examination and the required immunizations. Nobody may be hired in a food establishment without one.

On the job, food handlers wear clean working clothes. The cook wears a prescribed cap, and female staff wear caps or hairnets. Handlers keep good personal hygiene and wash their hands with soap and water, then dry them properly, right before work and after every toilet visit.
Memory aid: "Permit on the wall, certificate in the wallet, cap on the head, soap on the hands."
