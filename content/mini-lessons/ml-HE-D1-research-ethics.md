Teachers routinely collect test scores and observations to teach. Research adds ethical duties. If action-research data will be shared **outside the school community**, for example at a conference or in a journal, the teacher must obtain permission from **parents or guardians and the students**.

This is usually done with an **informed consent form**. It summarizes the study, describes the data to be collected, states that participation is **voluntary**, and promises **confidentiality or anonymity**. Unlike regular class tasks, students may decline to take part in the research.

Statistics texts likewise require informed consent from all participants. In the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act treats learners' education and health information as **sensitive personal information**, so research data must be protected and reported without identifying individual learners.
Memory aid: "Inform, Ask, Allow refusal, Anonymize."
Sources: SRC-0109 Ch. 11 Ethical issues; SRC-0091 1.4; SRC-0045 Sec. 3(l)
