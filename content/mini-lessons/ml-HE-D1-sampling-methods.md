How a sample is chosen decides whether results can be trusted. OpenStax describes four random methods and one common non-random one.
- **Simple random sample:** every member has an equal chance, like drawing names from a box.
- **Stratified sample:** split the population into groups called strata, such as grade levels, then take a proportionate random sample from **each** group.
- **Cluster sample:** split the population into clusters, such as sections, then randomly choose **some whole clusters** and include everyone in them.
- **Systematic sample:** pick a random starting point, then take **every nth** person from a list.
- **Convenience sample:** use whoever is easy to reach, such as the first learners who arrive at the canteen. It is not random and can be badly biased.

For a canteen survey, stratifying by grade level makes sure every grade is represented.
Memory aid: "Strata = Some from every group; Cluster = Choose whole groups; Systematic = every nth."
