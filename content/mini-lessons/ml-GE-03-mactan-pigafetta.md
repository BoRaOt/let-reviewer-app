On **April 27, 1521**, at **Mactan** in present-day Cebu, forces led by **Lapulapu** fought Spanish troops under **Ferdinand Magellan**, who was allied with Rajah **Humabon** of Cebu. **Magellan was killed**, and the Spanish expedition withdrew. Lapulapu is remembered as a hero of resistance to foreign intrusion.

Much of what we know comes from **Antonio Pigafetta**, an Italian chronicler on Magellan's expedition. His account, for example, gives the size of Lapulapu's force. As a primary source, it is valuable because Pigafetta was an eyewitness, but it is also told from the side of the expedition. Readers should notice whose perspective is missing: no written account survives from Lapulapu's people.

The battle is among the earliest recorded encounters between the islands' peoples and Europeans.
