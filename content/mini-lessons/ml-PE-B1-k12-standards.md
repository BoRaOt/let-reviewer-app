The **Enhanced Basic Education Act (RA 10533, 2013)** set up K to 12: at least **one year of kindergarten**, **six years of elementary**, and **six years of secondary**, made up of **four years of junior high** and **two years of senior high**. Kindergarten entrants are at least five; typical entry ages are six for Grade 1, twelve for junior high and sixteen for senior high.

**DepEd** designs the curriculum, coordinating with **CHED** and **TESDA** and consulting other stakeholders. Section 5 lists the standards the curriculum must meet. It must be learner-centered, inclusive and developmentally appropriate; relevant, responsive and research-based; culture-sensitive; contextualized and global; taught through constructivist, inquiry-based, reflective, collaborative and integrative approaches; built on **spiral progression**; and **flexible**, so schools can localize, indigenize and enhance it. A Curriculum Consultative Committee chaired by the DepEd Secretary reviews it.
