Our open entrepreneurship textbook describes an entrepreneurial mindset as curious and open. Entrepreneurs take calculated risks, meaning risks they have weighed against the possible reward, not reckless gambles. They are resourceful, making the most of whatever resources they have, and resilient, so they persevere through setbacks and crises. Starting a business also takes energy, decision-making skill, tenacity and flexibility. Costs matter just as much as attitude. A variable cost, such as ingredients for each pizza, goes up or down with the number of items made. A fixed cost, such as monthly stall rent, stays the same whatever the sales. In a business plan, the executive summary gives an overview of the whole plan. Write it last, because it summarizes everything else, but place it first in the document.
Memory aid: "Weigh the risk, use what you have, keep going."
