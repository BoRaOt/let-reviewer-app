The Consumer Act of the Philippines, Republic Act 7394 of 1992, declares a policy to protect consumers' interests, promote their welfare, and set standards of conduct for business and industry. It lists five objectives:
1. protection against hazards to health and safety;
2. protection against deceptive, unfair and unconscionable sales acts and practices;
3. information and education so consumers can choose well;
4. adequate rights and means of redress;
5. involving consumer representatives in social and economic policy.

Enforcement is split by product. The Department of Health handles food, drugs, cosmetics, devices and substances. The Department of Agriculture handles agricultural products. The Department of Trade and Industry handles other consumer products. A National Consumer Affairs Council coordinates consumer programs. On the exam, match the product to the right department.
Memory aid: "Health for what you eat or apply, Agriculture for what you grow, Trade for everything else."
