Republic Act 11650 of 2022 makes inclusion the policy of every early and basic education school, public and private. No learner may be denied admission because of a disability. The law defines inclusive education as responding to the diverse needs of all learners so that they can fully participate, be present and achieve. This involves accommodation, modification, adaptation and individualization of content, approaches, structures and strategies. Each learner with a disability may have an Individualized Education Plan. The IEP sets out the learner's present level, the services to be provided, and their duration and frequency. DepEd and local governments must set up at least one Inclusive Learning Resource Center in every city and municipality, and all existing SPED Centers are renamed as such centers. The centers run the Child Find System, which identifies, locates and evaluates learners with disabilities who are out of school, so they can be included.
Memory aid: "Find them (Child Find), plan for them (IEP), support them (ILRC)."
