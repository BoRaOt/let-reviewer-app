Managing income starts with a **budget**: list what comes in, sort expenses into categories such as housing, food and transport, and compare. Only then can you plan to save.

There are three common places to keep savings. A **savings account** lets you deposit and withdraw whenever you like, but it pays low interest, so money grows slowly. A **certificate of deposit** (called a time deposit in Philippine banks) pays more but locks the money for a set term. A **money market account** sits between the two.

Experts advise building an **emergency fund** that covers three to six months of living expenses. This protects the family if income stops or a sudden cost appears, so it does not have to borrow at high interest. In class, learners practise by tracking a weekly allowance and setting aside a fixed share first.
Memory aid: "Budget first, save first, keep 3 to 6 months safe."
