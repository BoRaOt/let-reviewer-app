The **Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act (RA 10121, 2010)** renamed the old NDCC as the **National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC)**.

The Council is chaired by the **Secretary of National Defense**. It has four vice chairs, one for each thematic area: the **DILG Secretary** for disaster **preparedness**; the **DSWD Secretary** for disaster **response**; the **DOST Secretary** for disaster **prevention and mitigation**; and the **NEDA Director-General** for **rehabilitation and recovery**.

The **Office of Civil Defense** runs the national DRRM program and prepares the national DRRM plan.

Money is set aside locally. Each LGU's **Local DRRM Fund** is **at least 5 percent** of its estimated revenue, and **30 percent** of that is a **Quick Response Fund** for relief and recovery.
