A family budget compares income with expenses so money goes where it should.
- **Fixed expenses** stay the same from month to month, such as rent, loan payments, insurance and internet.
- **Variable expenses** change, such as groceries and fuel.
- Bills paid yearly, quarterly or twice a year are spread out by dividing the bill by the number of months it covers.

Group expenses into categories, total the monthly income and expenses, and compare. If expenses are higher, make adjustments, and always plan for unexpected costs such as repairs. A simple guideline is the 50-30-20 philosophy: half of income goes to needs, thirty percent to wants, and twenty percent to savings and paying off debts. Its main lesson is that needs and wants are often confused. Basic needs include housing, utilities, groceries, health care and minimum debt payments.
Memory aid: "50 Needs, 30 Wants, 20 Savings."
