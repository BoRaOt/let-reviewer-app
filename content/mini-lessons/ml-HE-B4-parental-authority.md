---
Under the Family Code, the father and the mother exercise parental authority jointly over their common children. If they disagree, the text says the father's decision prevails unless a court orders otherwise. Children are expected to respect their parents and obey them while under parental authority. Parental authority also carries responsibility. Parents and others exercising it are civilly liable for injuries and damages caused by their unemancipated children who live with them, subject to the defenses the law allows. Parental authority never permits abuse. RA 7610 protects children, meaning persons below eighteen or older persons unable to protect themselves, from physical and psychological abuse, neglect, cruelty and acts that demean their dignity. A Home Economics teacher teaching parenting basics should present authority and protection together.
Memory aid: "Joint authority, joint responsibility — never abuse."
Sources: SRC-0071 Arts. 211, 221; SRC-0029 Sec. 3
