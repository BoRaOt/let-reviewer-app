**Juan Luna's *Spoliarium*** depicts dying gladiators; Luna spent eight months on the canvas. Luna submitted it to the **Exposición Nacional de Bellas Artes in Madrid in 1884**, where it won the **first gold medal**. At the same exposition, **Félix Resurrección Hidalgo** won a **silver** medal. At a gathering of Filipinos in Madrid, **José Rizal** toasted their success as "fresh proof of racial equality", because it showed colonized Filipinos could equal or surpass Spanish painters. Today the *Spoliarium* greets visitors at the **National Museum of Fine Arts** in Manila and is considered the largest painting in the country.

**Fernando Amorsolo** (1892–1972), the "**Grand Old Man of Philippine Art**", painted portraits and sunlit **rural landscapes** and the people around him, from farmers to city dwellers. He was the **first National Artist** of the Philippines, praised for his impressionistic technique and his signature **backlighting**, which makes figures glow against the light.
