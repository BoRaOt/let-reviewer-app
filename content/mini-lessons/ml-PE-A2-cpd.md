Passing the LET is a beginning, not an end. The Continuing Professional Development Act of 2016 (RA 10912) makes CPD a mandatory requirement for renewing the professional identification card of every professional regulated by the PRC, teachers included. CPD programs aim to upgrade professionals' competencies and qualifications in line with the Philippine Qualifications Framework, the ASEAN Qualifications Reference Framework, and ASEAN mutual recognition arrangements. In short, CPD keeps a license current and supports career progression and specialization. RA 7836 adds an older idea of growth: teachers may take an oral and written merit examination at least once every five years, free of charge, as an extra basis for merit promotion. Keep the two apart in your head. CPD is tied to renewing the professional ID. The merit exam is optional and is used for promotion.
Memory aid: "CPD renews the ID; the merit exam promotes."
Sources: SRC-0030 Secs. 4, 10; SRC-0016 Sec. 19
