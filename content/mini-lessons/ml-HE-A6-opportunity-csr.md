Many people have business ideas, but not every idea is worth pursuing. An idea becomes an entrepreneurial opportunity at the point where there is identifiable consumer demand and it is feasible to satisfy that demand. A great recipe is an idea. It becomes an opportunity when you find customers who want it and you can make and sell it at a workable cost. Spotting opportunities starts with the right mindset: pay close attention to customer needs and to trends in the market. Entrepreneurs also carry responsibilities. Corporate social responsibility, or CSR, is the practice of a business seeing itself as a member of society with social obligations and environmental concerns. Obeying the law is not the same as being ethical, because the law does not settle every ethical dilemma. A responsible business aims to do good while doing well.
Memory aid: "Demand plus doable equals opportunity."
Sources: SRC-0098 sections 3.2 and 5.1
