Two recent laws extend consumer protection to new ways of buying.
- **Internet Transactions Act of 2023 (Republic Act 11967):** covers business-to-business and business-to-consumer internet transactions under the Department of Trade and Industry. It applies when one party is in the Philippines, or when the digital platform, online retailer or merchant targets the Philippine market. It does not cover online media content, or transactions between private consumers, known as consumer-to-consumer sales.
- **Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection Act (Republic Act 11765, 2022):** protects financial consumers, meaning anyone who buys, leases or receives, or may buy, lease or receive, financial products or services such as savings and deposits from a financial service provider.

These laws matter for family resource management, because families now shop, save and borrow online.
Memory aid: "B2B and B2C online = covered; C2C = not covered."
Sources: SRC-0069 Sec. 3; SRC-0070 Sec. 3
