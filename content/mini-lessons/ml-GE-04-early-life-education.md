**José Protasio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda** was born on **June 19, 1861**, in **Calamba, Laguna**. He was the **seventh child** of **Francisco Rizal Mercado** and **Teodora Alonso Realonda**, and was baptized three days later in the town parish.

He studied at the **Ateneo Municipal de Manila**, earning a bachelor of arts; before joining Ateneo he knew little Spanish. He then studied at the **University of Santo Tomas**. In **November 1882** he enrolled at the **Universidad Central de Madrid**, where he completed medicine. He became an **ophthalmologist**, an eye doctor, and later used the newly invented ophthalmoscope to operate on his mother's eye.

Rizal was a **polymath**: a writer, physician, linguist and artist. His years in Europe shaped his ideas about reform and drew him into the **Propaganda Movement**.

A common confusion: Calamba, not Manila, is his birthplace; Bagumbayan is where he died.
