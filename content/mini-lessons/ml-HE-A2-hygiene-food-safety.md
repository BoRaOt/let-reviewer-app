Good hygiene at home is mostly about stopping germs from travelling. Germs move from hands to food, from raw food to cooked food, and from one person to another. You cannot see, smell or taste most bacteria, so habits matter more than looks.

The four key steps are **clean, separate, cook and chill**. Wash hands with warm, soapy water for at least twenty seconds before preparing food and every time after touching raw meat, poultry or fish. Wash fruits and vegetables, even ones you will peel. Use separate cutting boards for produce and raw meat, and sanitize boards with about one teaspoon of bleach in a litre of water.

Never thaw frozen meat on the counter at room temperature, because that is the temperature bacteria like best. Thaw it in the refrigerator, the microwave, or a sealed bag in cold water.
Memory aid: "Clean, Separate, Cook, Chill — and twenty seconds of soap."
