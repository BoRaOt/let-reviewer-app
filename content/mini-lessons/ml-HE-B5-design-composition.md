Whether you are making a wall hanging, arranging a bulletin board or decorating a room, the same building blocks apply. The **elements of design** are **line** (the path the eye follows), **shape** (flat areas, geometric or organic), **form** (three-dimensional volume), **color**, **texture** (how a surface feels or seems to feel), **value** (lightness and darkness) and **space** (positive areas filled by objects and negative areas around them).

**Composition** is how the elements are arranged. Common techniques include the **rule of thirds** (place the main subject along lines that divide the space into thirds), the rule of odds (groups of three or five look natural), leaving space in the direction a subject faces, simplifying clutter, and using symmetry or movement to guide the eye.

For color, choose a scheme: monochromatic for calm, analogous for harmony, complementary for contrast.
Memory aid: "Line, Shape, Form, Color, Texture, Value, Space — then arrange by thirds."
