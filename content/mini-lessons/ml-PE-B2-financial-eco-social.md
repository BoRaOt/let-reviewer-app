The new literacies stretch "literacy" beyond reading and writing.

**Financial literacy** is the set of skills, knowledge, behaviors and attitudes that let a person make **informed decisions about money**. People with financial training tend to make better choices about budgeting, saving and borrowing. In HE classes, a family budget project builds it directly.

**Ecological literacy**, or ecoliteracy, is understanding the natural systems that make life on Earth possible and using ecological principles to build **sustainable communities**. Educator **David Orr** and physicist **Fritjof Capra** developed the idea in the 1990s. Waste segregation and school gardens are classroom examples.

**Social literacy**, from a sociocultural view, is literacy learned through **social interaction** with peers and adults. Books, games, conversations, field trips and stories, shared between school, family and community, all build it.
