subtopic: Performance, alternative and authentic assessment

In a **performance assessment**, learners carry out a complex task while the teacher judges both the **process** and the **product**. In Home Economics, that might be preparing a meal, sewing a blouse or setting a table. The teacher watches knife handling and sanitation, not only the finished dish.

Two related terms differ. **Alternative assessment** means any task that is not pencil-and-paper. **Authentic assessment** means a task close to what people do in the real world. Authenticity is a matter of degree: running the school canteen for a day is highly authentic; matching cooking terms to definitions is not.

Performance assessments assess complex outcomes, show learners the goal clearly, and usually have strong content validity. Their drawbacks are that they take a lot of time, so fewer can be given, and they are hard to score reliably unless the teacher uses clear rubrics.
Memory aid: "Process + Product; Real world = Authentic; Not paper = Alternative."
