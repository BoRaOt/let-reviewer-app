TESDA's Beauty Care standard splits a manicure or pedicure into four steps.
1. **Prepare the client.** Ask what nail service they want and check the condition of their nails. Give protective materials and use sanitized tools. If you see a nail disorder, recognize it and refer the client to the right person. Do not treat it yourself.
2. **Clean the nails.** Remove old polish, soak the nails in warm soapy water, push back and remove cuticles and dry skin, then trim and file to the shape the client wants. If you accidentally cut the client, apply antiseptic right away.
3. **Apply polish.** Start with a base coat in long strokes, beginning at the little finger. Then apply the color from the base of the nail to its edge, and finish with a top coat.
4. **Final retouch.** Remove excess polish, apply a quick-drying agent, and advise on nail care.
Memory aid: "Prep, Clean, Polish, Retouch", with the layers going B-C-T: Base, Color, Top.
