**Noli Me Tángere**, Latin for **"Touch Me Not"**, is Rizal's first novel. He finished it in **March 1887**. His friend **Máximo Viola** lent him money, so the book was printed in **Berlin** that year.

The hero is **Crisóstomo Ibarra**, a young mestizo who returns to the town of **San Diego, Laguna**, after **seven years** of study in Europe. He hopes to build a school and improve his town, but he clashes with powerful friars and officials, and his plans collapse. Through the story, Rizal exposed abuses in colonial society, especially by the friars, and the sufferings of ordinary Filipinos.

Spanish authorities in the Philippines **banned** the book, but copies were smuggled into the country. The Rizal Law now requires students to read it in unexpurgated form.
