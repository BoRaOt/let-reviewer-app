# PE-A2 — The professional teacher: licensure, rights and protective laws

The official outline asks you to describe the professional teacher and the high standards of a teacher's personal and professional life, and to explain the roles of a teacher, "with responsibilities as specified in the Code of Ethics for Professional Teachers" [SRC-0001, ProfEd Section A, outcome 2.3]. This lesson covers the laws that make teaching a regulated profession and protect teachers and learners. The Code of Ethics and the PPST get their own lessons once their official texts are in hand (see "Pending" below).

## 1. Plain explanation

**Teaching is a licensed profession.** RA 7836 (1994) is the Philippine Teachers Professionalization Act. It created the **Board for Professional Teachers**, which works under the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC). Nobody may practice as a professional teacher without a certificate of registration and a professional license, unless the law allows an exception such as a special or temporary permit. The LET (officially the LEPT) is the written exam that leads to that license. For secondary teachers it has three parts: general education, professional education and the field of specialization. The law states its aim plainly: the State ensures quality education by regulating the licensure exam and the practice of teaching.

**Who may take the exam** depends on the degree. RA 9293 (2004) changed the rule for graduates outside education: they need at least **18 units** of professional education (the original RA 7836 said 10).

**Public school teachers have a charter of rights**, RA 4670 (1966), the *Magna Carta for Public School Teachers*. It covers:
- workload (6 hours of actual classroom teaching a day)
- job security, consent before transfer, and fair disciplinary procedure
- no discrimination, academic freedom in teaching methods
- fair salaries and no unauthorized salary deductions
- free medical examination, study leave, and the right to organize

**Teachers must keep learning.** RA 10912 (2016) makes continuing professional development (CPD) a requirement for renewing the professional ID.

**Schools must protect learners**, and teachers are part of that system:
- RA 10627 (Anti-Bullying Act) requires every elementary and secondary school to adopt an anti-bullying policy.
- RA 7877 (Anti-Sexual Harassment Act) and RA 11313 (Safe Spaces Act) cover harassment in schools, including by teachers who hold authority over students.
- RA 7610 protects children from abuse.
- RA 9262 covers violence against women and their children, which a teacher may see in a learner's home situation.

## 2. Testable facts

**Licensure (RA 7836 as amended)**
- RA 7836's policy: the State recognizes the vital role of teachers in nation-building and ensures quality education by supervising the licensure exam and professionalizing teaching. [SRC-0016, Sec. 2]
- The Board for Professional Teachers is a collegial body under the general supervision and administrative control of the PRC. It has **5 members** appointed by the President from PRC recommendees chosen from nominees of the accredited teachers' association. [SRC-0016, Sec. 5]
- Board members serve **3-year** terms; a person who has served **two consecutive terms** cannot be reappointed. [SRC-0016, Sec. 7]
- A Board member must be at least **35 years old**, a citizen and resident, of proven integrity, with an education degree. [SRC-0016, Sec. 8]
- Board duties include issuing, suspending or revoking certificates of registration, fixing exam dates and places, and prescribing or adopting a code of ethical and professional standards. [SRC-0016, Sec. 6]
- The written exam must be given **at least once a year**; a valid certificate of registration and professional license are required to practice. [SRC-0016, Sec. 13]
- Secondary-level exam = **three parts**: professional education, general education, field of specialization; elementary = two parts (professional and general education). [SRC-0016, Sec. 14]
- Applicants must be at least **18 years old**, of good health and reputation with high moral values, and not convicted by final judgment of an offense involving moral turpitude. [SRC-0016, Sec. 15]
- The Board reports exam ratings to the PRC within **120 days** after the exam. [SRC-0016, Sec. 16]
- Registration starts when the name is entered in the **roster of professional teachers**; every registrant takes a **professional oath** before practicing. [SRC-0016, Secs. 17–18]
- Teachers may take an optional oral and written **merit examination at least once in 5 years**, free of charge, as a basis for merit promotion. [SRC-0016, Sec. 19]
- After notice and hearing, the Board may suspend or revoke a certificate for causes such as criminal conviction or immoral, unprofessional or dishonorable conduct. [SRC-0016, Sec. 23]
- Practicing without a certificate is punishable by a fine of **P5,000–P20,000** or imprisonment of **6 months to 5 years**, or both. [SRC-0016, Sec. 28]
- RA 9293: graduates of arts and sciences need at least **18 units** of professional education to qualify (originally 10 units under RA 7836). [SRC-0017, Sec. 1; SRC-0016, Sec. 15(e)(3)]
- RA 9293: teachers of vocational and two-year technical courses need a bachelor's degree in their field plus at least **18 units** of professional education. [SRC-0017, Sec. 1]
- RA 9293: registered teachers who have **not practiced for 5 years** must take at least **12 units** of education courses before returning. [SRC-0017, Sec. 2]
- The professional boards prepare the syllabi or TOS, and exam questions must stay strictly within them. [SRC-0018, Sec. 9(h)]

**Magna Carta for Public School Teachers (RA 4670)**
- Minimum qualification for secondary teachers: a bachelor's degree in education with a major and a minor, or an arts or science degree with at least 18 professional units. [SRC-0019, Sec. 3]
- A teacher hired on provisional status for lack of civil service eligibility serves a probation of **at least one year**. [SRC-0019, Sec. 4]
- Security of tenure is assured. A provisional teacher becomes permanent after **at least 10 years** of continuous, efficient, faithful service. [SRC-0019, Sec. 5]
- No transfer **without the teacher's consent**, except for cause. If the service requires a transfer, the superintendent must first notify the teacher of the reasons, and the teacher may appeal. [SRC-0019, Sec. 6]
- Disciplinary safeguards: right to be **informed in writing** of the charges, full access to the evidence, to defend oneself with a representative of choice, and to appeal. No publicity while the case is pending. [SRC-0019, Sec. 8]
- Administrative charges are first heard by a **committee** chaired by the Schools Division Superintendent (or a representative of at least division-supervisor rank), with a teachers' organization representative and a division supervisor. [SRC-0019, Sec. 9]
- No discrimination in entering, practicing or leaving the profession on grounds other than professional ones. [SRC-0019, Sec. 10]
- Married couples who are both public school teachers should, whenever possible, be employed in the same locality. [SRC-0019, Sec. 11]
- Teachers enjoy **academic freedom** in their professional duties, especially in teaching and classroom methods. [SRC-0019, Sec. 12]
- **Teaching hours:** at most **6 hours** of actual classroom teaching a day. Up to **8 hours** when the service requires it, paid at the regular rate **plus at least 25%** of basic pay. [SRC-0019, Sec. 13]
- Co-curricular and out-of-school work beyond normal duties, after 6 hours of teaching, earns at least **25%** additional compensation. [SRC-0019, Sec. 14]
- No salary deductions except as authorized by law. With the teacher's written authority, teachers' association dues and insurance premiums may be deducted. [SRC-0019, Sec. 21]
- Compulsory **free medical examination** before starting to teach, repeated **at least once a year**. [SRC-0019, Sec. 22]
- Physical and nervous strain on the teacher's health is recognized as a compensable occupational disease. [SRC-0019, Sec. 23]
- **Study leave:** up to **one school year** after **7 years** of service, at **at least 60%** of monthly salary. [SRC-0019, Sec. 24]
- **Indefinite sick leave** when an illness needs treatment exceeding one year. [SRC-0019, Sec. 25]
- On retirement, a **one-range salary raise** is the basis for computing retirement benefits. [SRC-0019, Sec. 26]
- Teachers may freely **establish and join organizations** without prior authorization. Making employment conditional on not joining is prohibited discrimination. [SRC-0019, Secs. 27–28]

**Continuing professional development (RA 10912)**
- CPD is a **mandatory requirement** for renewing the professional identification card of every PRC-regulated professional. [SRC-0030, Sec. 10]
- CPD programs aim to upgrade competencies in line with the Philippine Qualifications Framework (PQF), the ASEAN Qualifications Reference Framework (AQRF) and ASEAN mutual recognition arrangements. [SRC-0030, Sec. 4]

**Protecting learners**
- **Bullying** (RA 10627) is any severe or repeated use by one or more students of written, verbal or electronic expression, or a physical act or gesture, directed at another student, causing fear of harm, a hostile environment, infringement of rights, or disruption of the educational process. [SRC-0025, Sec. 2]
- **All elementary and secondary schools** must adopt anti-bullying policies, including bans on bullying on and near school grounds, at school activities, through school technology, and **retaliation** against those who report. [SRC-0025, Sec. 3]
- The **school principal** (or equivalent) is responsible for implementing anti-bullying policies. School staff, students, parents and volunteers must report bullying they witness. [SRC-0025, Sec. 4]
- Schools report their anti-bullying policies in writing to the **schools division superintendent** within 6 months of the law taking effect, then report bullying statistics every first week of the school year. [SRC-0025, Sec. 5]
- **Education-related sexual harassment** (RA 7877) is committed by a teacher, instructor, professor, coach or trainer who, having **authority, influence or moral ascendancy**, demands, requests or requires a sexual favor, **whether or not the demand is accepted**. [SRC-0026, Sec. 3]
- The **Safe Spaces Act** (RA 11313) requires every school, public or private, to designate an officer to receive complaints and to adopt and publish grievance procedures. [SRC-0027, Sec. 21]
- School heads must post the Safe Spaces Act, run prevention measures, and create an independent mechanism to investigate complaints. [SRC-0027, Sec. 22]
- **Children** (RA 7610) means persons **below 18**, or older persons unable to protect themselves because of a physical or mental disability or condition. [SRC-0029, Sec. 3(a)]
- **Child abuse** includes psychological and physical abuse, neglect, cruelty, sexual abuse and emotional maltreatment, and acts that debase or demean a child's dignity. [SRC-0029, Sec. 3(b)]
- **VAWC** (RA 9262) covers physical, sexual and psychological harm and economic abuse by a husband, former husband, or a man the woman had a sexual or dating relationship or a common child with, against the woman or her child. [SRC-0028, Sec. 3(a)]

**The Constitution on teachers**
- The State shall enhance the right of teachers to professional advancement. [SRC-0007, Art. XIV Sec. 5(4)]
- The State shall give the **highest budgetary priority** to education and ensure that teaching attracts and retains its rightful share of the best talents. [SRC-0007, Art. XIV Sec. 5(5)]

## 3. Common mistakes

- **RA 7836 vs RA 9293 vs RA 4670.** RA 7836 = licensure and the Board. RA 9293 = amended licensure eligibility (18 units; 12-unit refresher). RA 4670 = rights of *public* school teachers. A question about "who may take the LET" is never RA 4670.
- **"RA 4670 gives free legal assistance."** The text of RA 4670 contains no such provision. Do not choose it on that basis.
- **6 vs 8 hours.** The cap is 6 hours of *actual classroom teaching*. 8 hours is the absolute maximum, allowed only when the service requires it and only with extra pay of at least 25%.
- **Study leave numbers.** It comes after 7 years of service, lasts at most one school year, and pays at least 60% of salary. Swapping 7 and 10 (the years to permanent status) is a classic trap.
- **Board size and terms.** 5 members, 3-year terms, no more than two consecutive terms. Board members must be at least 35; examinees at least 18.
- **Bullying is student-to-student** under RA 10627's definition. A teacher harassing a student falls under RA 7877 or RA 11313, or child abuse under RA 7610, not the Anti-Bullying Act.
- **Sexual harassment needs no acceptance and no physical contact.** RA 7877 is committed by the demand or request itself, whether or not it is accepted.
- **CPD** is tied to renewing the professional ID card, not to taking the LET.

## 4. Memory aid

**"5-3-35, 6-8-25, 7-1-60"**
- **Board:** 5 members, 3-year terms, members at least 35.
- **Teaching day:** 6 hours normal, 8 at most, +25% pay for the extra.
- **Study leave:** after 7 years, for 1 school year, at 60% pay.
