Scientists learned Earth's inside from **seismic waves**. The thin outer **crust** sits on the thick **mantle**. Below is a **liquid outer core** and a **solid inner core**. Oceanic crust, about 6 kilometers thick and made of basalt, covers 55 percent of the surface.

Rocks come in three kinds. **Igneous** rock forms when molten material cools; all volcanic rock is igneous, and basalt and granite are the main crustal rocks. **Sedimentary** rock forms from fragments or shells laid down by wind or water and cemented together. **Metamorphic** rock is older rock changed by heat and pressure.

**Plate tectonics** explains how slow currents in the mantle move huge plates of crust. Plates pull apart at **rift zones**. At **subduction zones** one plate sinks beneath another, a process linked to deep trenches and volcanic activity.
