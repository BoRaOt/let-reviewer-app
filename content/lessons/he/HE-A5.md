# HE-A5 — Common competencies

Annex F gives the exploratory "common competencies" **6 items** (Remember 1, Understand 3, Apply 1, Analyze 1). The outcomes are:
- select, use and maintain tools and equipment;
- perform basic mensuration and calculations;
- read and interpret plans and drawings;
- practice occupational health, safety and ethical values in the workplace;
- apply communication, teamwork, work ethics and problem solving.

[SRC-0002, Annex F, A.5]. These are the skills shared by every TLE area, so TESDA training regulations repeat them across trades.

## 1. Plain explanation

**Safety comes first, by law.** The OSH Standards Act (RA 11058, 2018) makes employers furnish a workplace free of hazards and give safety instructions. It gives workers four rights:
- the right to **know** the hazards;
- the right to **refuse** imminently dangerous work;
- the right to **report** accidents and hazards;
- the right to **free PPE** (personal protective equipment).

Workplaces must post safety signage.

**TESDA's OHS unit** (in Cookery, Housekeeping and other TRs) teaches workers to **identify, evaluate and control** hazards, **maintain awareness** through drills, and **give basic first aid**. Hazards are grouped as **physical, biological, chemical and ergonomic**.

**Mensuration and calculation** means choosing the right measuring tool, measuring accurately, and computing with the four operations, fractions, percentages, unit conversion and area or volume formulas.

**Tool maintenance** means checking condition, setting defective tools aside with labels, doing preventive maintenance, and storing tools properly.

**Technical drawing** means picking the right drawing for the job, reading its dimensions and symbols, and checking it against the job requirements.

## 2. Testable facts

**OSH law (RA 11058)**
- Employers must provide a workplace **free from hazardous conditions**, give **complete job safety instructions or orientation** (especially to new workers), inform workers of hazards and preventive measures, and use only **approved devices and equipment**. [SRC-0063, Sec. 4(a)]
- **Right to know:** workers must be informed of all workplace hazards and given training on chemical, electrical, mechanical and ergonomic safety. [SRC-0063, Sec. 5]
- **Right to refuse unsafe work:** without threat or reprisal, when DOLE determines that an **imminent danger** exists and the employer has not corrected it. [SRC-0063, Sec. 6]
- **Right to report** accidents, dangerous occurrences and hazards to the employer, DOLE and other authorities. [SRC-0063, Sec. 7]
- **Right to PPE:** employers provide protective equipment **free of charge** (eyes, face, hands, feet, lifelines and harnesses, respirators, shields) when the work or environment is hazardous. [SRC-0063, Sec. 8]
- **Safety signage and devices** must be posted prominently, in a language understandable to all, following DOLE standards. [SRC-0063, Sec. 9]
- The cost of an approved safety and health program is **part of operating cost**, and a separate pay item in construction contracts. [SRC-0063, Sec. 20]

**TESDA OHS unit ("Practice occupational health and safety procedures")**
- Elements: (1) identify hazards and risks; (2) evaluate hazards and risks; (3) control hazards and risks; (4) maintain OHS awareness; (5) perform basic first-aid procedures. [SRC-0075, OHS unit]
- Evaluating hazards uses **maximum tolerable limits** based on **threshold limit values (TLV)**. [SRC-0075, OHS unit, element 2.1]
- Hazard types: **physical** (impact, illumination, pressure, noise, vibration, temperature, radiation); **biological** (bacteria, viruses, plants, parasites, mites, molds, fungi, insects); **chemical** (dusts, fibers, mists, fumes, smoke, gases, vapors); **ergonomic** (over-exertion, awkward or static positions, fatigue, monotony, among others). [SRC-0075, OHS unit, range of variables 2]
- **Contingency measures** include evacuation, isolation, decontamination, and calling emergency personnel. [SRC-0075, OHS unit, range of variables 3]
- **PPE** examples: mask, gloves, goggles, hairnet, cap or bonnet, face shield, ear muffs, apron, gown or coverall, anti-static suit. [SRC-0075, OHS unit, range of variables 4]
- **Drills and training** include fire drill, earthquake drill, basic life support or CPR, first aid, spillage control, and disaster preparedness. [SRC-0075, OHS unit, range of variables 5]

**Mensuration and calculation (TESDA "Perform mensurations and calculations")**
- Select measuring instruments: identify the object's **regular geometric shape**, choose tools for the job, and get correct specifications; alternative tools may be used **without sacrificing cost and quality**. [SRC-0084, mensuration unit, element 1]
- Carry out measurements and calculations: obtain accurate measurements; use the **four basic operations**; use **fractions, percentages and mixed numbers**; **self-check** computations for accuracy. [SRC-0084, mensuration unit, element 2]
- Required knowledge: **linear and geometrical measurement**; trade mathematics including **unit conversion, ratio and proportion, and area**; formulas for volume, area and perimeter of plane and solid figures. [SRC-0084, mensuration unit, required knowledge and skills]

**Tools and equipment (TESDA "Maintain tools and equipment")**
- Check condition: identify tools by classification and job; **segregate and label non-functional tools**; observe manufacturers' instructions for tool safety; check the condition of PPE. [SRC-0084, maintain tools unit, element 1]
- Underpinning knowledge includes safety practices (use of PPE, handling of tools, **good housekeeping**) and the types and uses of **lubricants** and **cleaning materials**. [SRC-0084, maintain tools unit, required knowledge]

**Plans and drawings (TESDA "Prepare and interpret technical drawing")**
- Identify the right technical drawing for the job and sort drawings by type. [SRC-0082, technical drawing unit, element 1]
- Interpret: recognize components, identify the **dimensions** of key features, interpret **symbols** correctly, and **check and validate** the drawing against job requirements. [SRC-0082, technical drawing unit, element 2]
- Underpinning knowledge: trade mathematics (linear measurement, dimension, unit conversion), **blueprint reading and plan specification** (architectural, electrical, electronic, mechanical plans; symbols and abbreviations; drawing standards), and **orthographic drawing**. [SRC-0082, technical drawing unit, required knowledge and skills]

## 3. Common mistakes

- **Right to refuse is not unconditional.** It applies to an **imminent danger** the employer has not corrected, as determined by DOLE.
- **PPE is free.** The employer pays, not the worker.
- **Hazard categories.** Noise and heat are *physical*; molds and bacteria are *biological*; fumes and dust are *chemical*; awkward posture and repetitive strain are *ergonomic*.
- **Evaluate vs control.** Measuring exposure against TLVs = *evaluate*. Following procedures and using PPE = *control*.
- **Defective tools** are segregated and labeled, not left in the tool box "for later".

## 4. Memory aid

**OHS cycle "I-E-C-M-F"** = Identify, Evaluate, Control, Maintain awareness, First aid.
**Worker rights "Know, No, Tell, Gear"** = right to know, right to refuse, right to report, right to PPE.
