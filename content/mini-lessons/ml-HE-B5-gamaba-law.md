The **Manlilikha ng Bayan Act (RA 7355, 1992)** honors traditional folk artists whose skills keep a community's art alive. A **Manlilikha ng Bayan** is a Filipino, or group, practicing a traditional art uniquely Filipino at a very high level and passing it on to the present generation.

Candidates must meet five criteria: **technical and creative skill**, **artistic quality**, **community tradition** (they have taught or will teach others), a **folk art tradition documented for at least 50 years**, and **character and integrity**.

Awardees receive a plaque or medal, **₱100,000 at first and ₱10,000 a month for life**, documentation of their works, and possibly a vocational course built on their methods and a DTI study to turn their art into a **cottage industry**. The award is run by the **NCCA** and follows UNESCO's idea of Living Human Treasures. Categories include weaving, carving, pottery, fiber arts and, since 2020, ethnomedicine.
