**Multicultural education** is a set of strategies that teach students about the histories, cultures and contributions of diverse groups, and promote inclusion, diversity and democracy. **James Banks** describes five dimensions teachers should work on.
1. **Content integration:** using examples and content from many cultures.
2. **Knowledge construction:** helping students see how cultural assumptions and biases shape what a field counts as knowledge.
3. **Prejudice reduction:** using methods and materials that change students' attitudes toward other groups.
4. **Equity pedagogy:** changing teaching methods so students from diverse racial, cultural, gender and social-class groups can achieve.
5. **Empowering school culture:** examining grouping, labeling, sports and staff–student interaction so the whole school empowers every learner.

In a Philippine class, featuring Maranao, Ilocano and T'boli dishes in a cookery unit is content integration; adjusting instruction for IP learners is equity pedagogy.
