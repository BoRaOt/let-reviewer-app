**Socialization** is how people learn the norms, values and behaviors of their society, and it shapes the self. It happens through **agents of socialization**.

The **family is the first agent**. Parents, siblings and relatives teach language, values and roles long before school, and in Filipino homes extended relatives often share this role.

**Peer groups**, people of similar age and status, matter more as children grow, especially in adolescence; they teach how to fit in and can challenge family values.

The **media** fills us with messages about norms and expectations, from advertising to social media.

**Formal institutions**, such as **schools, workplaces and government**, teach people how to behave within and navigate those systems. School teaches not only lessons but routines like waiting your turn and respecting authority.

A teacher who understands these agents can see why learners arrive with very different selves.
