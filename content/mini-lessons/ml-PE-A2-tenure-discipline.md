RA 4670 protects a public school teacher's job. Security of tenure is assured. A teacher hired on provisional status for lack of civil service eligibility serves a probation of at least one year, and becomes permanent after at least ten years of continuous, efficient and faithful service. A teacher cannot be transferred without consent except for cause. When the service requires a transfer, the superintendent must first explain the reasons, and the teacher may appeal. If charges are filed, the teacher has the right to be informed in writing, to see all the evidence, to defend themselves with a representative of their choice, and to appeal. The case must not be publicized while pending. The first hearing is held by a committee chaired by the Schools Division Superintendent, with a teachers' organization representative and a division supervisor.
