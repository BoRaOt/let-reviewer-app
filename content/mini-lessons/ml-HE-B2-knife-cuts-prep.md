Knife cuts differ by shape and size. Julienne means long, thin strips like matchsticks, as with carrots for a salad. Dicing means cutting into small, even cubes so that pieces cook at the same rate. Mincing means cutting into very small, uniform pieces, finer than a dice, as with garlic for sautéing. Before cooking, a cook may marinate meat by soaking it in a seasoned, often acidic liquid such as vinegar or calamansi juice, which adds flavor. In baking, creaming means beating softened butter with sugar so that air bubbles are trapped in the fat, giving a light and fluffy mixture that helps the cake rise. Kneading means working bread dough so gluten forms and the dough becomes strong and elastic. Each term names one clear action.
Memory aid: "Strips, Cubes, Crumbs" = julienne, dice, mince.
