The **Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)** protects information about people, called data subjects. **Personal information** is anything that identifies a person, alone or combined with other data. **Sensitive personal information** gets extra protection: race, ethnic origin, marital status, **age**, religion and political affiliation; **health and education** records; and government-issued details such as SSS numbers and tax returns.

Processing must follow three principles: **transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality**. Consent must be freely given, specific and informed, and recorded in writing, electronically or by recording.

Data subjects have rights: to be **informed**, to **access** their data, to **correct** errors, to have wrong or unlawfully obtained data **blocked or removed**, and to be **paid damages**. The **National Privacy Commission** enforces the law.

For teachers, this means learners' grades, health notes and family details must not be posted or shared without a lawful basis.
