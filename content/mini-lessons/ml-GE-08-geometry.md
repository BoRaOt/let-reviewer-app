**Triangles:** the three interior angles always add up to **180°**. If two angles are 50° and 60°, the third is 70°. In a **right triangle**, the side opposite the 90° angle is the **hypotenuse**, the longest side, and the **Pythagorean theorem** holds: a² + b² = c². A ladder 5 m long with its foot 3 m from a wall reaches 4 m up, since 3² + 4² = 5².

**Area formulas:**
- Rectangle: A = l × w.
- Triangle: A = ½ × b × h.
- Circle: A = πr², and circumference C = 2πr.

**Volume of a prism:** V = B × h, the area of the base times the height. A box 30 cm by 20 cm by 10 cm holds 6,000 cm³, which is 6 liters.

Always check units: area is in square units, volume in cubic units. A floor 4 m by 3 m needs 12 m² of tiles.
Memory aid: "180 inside, a² + b² = c², area squared, volume cubed."
