Three laws frame art and culture in the Philippines.

**RA 7356** created the **National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA)**. It treats culture as a human right and states that **every citizen has a duty to preserve and conserve** Filipino historical and cultural heritage and resources.

**RA 10066**, the National Cultural Heritage Act, defines **cultural property** as all products of human creativity through which a people and nation reveal their identity, whether tangible or intangible, and protects declared National Cultural Treasures and Important Cultural Properties.

**RA 8293**, the Intellectual Property Code, gives creators **moral rights** that exist apart from economic rights. An author may require that the work be **attributed** to them, may alter or withhold the work before publication, and may **object to distortion or mutilation** that would harm their honor or reputation.

In class, crediting the artist of every image used is respect for moral rights.
