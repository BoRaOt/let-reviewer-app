# HE-B3 — Food service management (school food service)

Annex F gives this line **6 items** (Understand 1, Apply 3, Analyze 1, Evaluate 1). Outcome: **school food service** — preparing, managing and serving in the school cafeteria, and caring for and safely using tools, supplies and equipment. [SRC-0002, Annex F, B.3]

A school canteen is a **food establishment** under the Code on Sanitation, a **food business** under the Food Safety Act (with the **LGU** responsible for school canteens), and the site of the **school-based feeding program** under RA 11037. HE-B7 covers sanitary permits, health certificates and food-handler dress; this lesson covers washing, sanitizing, storage, service, and the feeding program.

## 1. Plain explanation

**Dishwashing has three stages:** scrape and pre-rinse; wash in warm soapy water; then **sanitize** (hot water, chlorine, steam or hot air). Air-dry on racks instead of wiping with cloths. Store cups and glasses **upside down**, off the lowest shelves.

**Storage:** dry goods in covered containers on shelves in a cool room; perishables in the refrigerator at **7 °C or below**, each kind at its own range.

**Serving:** do not touch food or the eating surfaces of utensils; serve with tools, not fingers; keep clean and soiled linens apart.

**Service styles:** American (plate), Russian (platter), buffet. A cafeteria is a self-service line.

**Feeding program (RA 11037):** DepEd gives at least **one fortified meal** a day for at least **120 days** a year to undernourished public school children from kindergarten to Grade 6.

## 2. Testable facts

**Washing and sanitizing utensils (PD 856)**
- Equipment and utensils must be easy to clean and safe; **lead-soldered containers and cadmium-lined piping** must not be used; food-contact surfaces must be impervious, corrosion-resistant, non-toxic, easy to clean, durable and chip-resistant. [SRC-0059, Sec. 23]
- Washing: **scrape and pre-rinse**; wash thoroughly in **warm water at 49 °C (120 °F)** with soap or detergent; change wash water often if running water is not used. [SRC-0059, Sec. 24]
- Bactericidal treatment after washing, one of: immersion **at least 30 seconds in water at 77 °C (170 °F)** or more; immersion **at least 1 minute in lukewarm 50 ppm chlorine** solution; steam cabinet; or oven/hot-air cabinet **at 82 °C (180 °F) for at least 20 minutes**; or another method approved by the local health authority. [SRC-0059, Sec. 25]
- Washed utensils **drain-dry on wire racks without drying cloths**, or are stored self-draining for air-drying. [SRC-0059, Sec. 26(a)]
- Store utensils in a clean, dry place protected from vermin; **cups, bowls and glasses are inverted**; utensils are not stored on the **bottom shelves** of open cabinets below the working top. [SRC-0059, Sec. 27]

**Storage (PD 856)**
- Dry, non-perishable foods: designated spaces and shelves; containers of **metal with tight covers**; recommended dry-store temperature about **10–15 °C**. [SRC-0059, Sec. 28]
- Perishable foods are kept **at or below 7 °C (45 °F)** except during preparation or when held for immediate serving. [SRC-0059, Sec. 29(a)]
- Recommended ranges: **meat and fish 0–3 °C**; **milk and milk products 5–7 °C**; **fruits and vegetables 7–10 °C** (stored in cool rooms). [SRC-0059, Sec. 29(c)–(d)]
- Refrigerators must be clean, in good repair, odor-free, have **thermometers**, and enough shelving to avoid overstocking and allow ventilation. [SRC-0059, Sec. 29(e)]

**Food service hygiene (PD 856)**
- **Avoid hand contact** with food; fingers are not used to serve butter, ice and similar items; sugar is served in covered dispensers or single-service packets. [SRC-0059, Sec. 30(a)]
- The surfaces of glasses and tableware that touch food or the mouth are **not handled**; single-service items are stored in sanitary cartons and handled without touching the food-contact surface. [SRC-0059, Sec. 30(b)–(c)]
- Clean linens are stored in designated clean places; **soiled linens go into a closed, marked bin**. [SRC-0059, Sec. 30(d)]
- Dippers and scoops for frozen desserts are kept in **running water** or water at 77 °C, or washed and dried after each use. [SRC-0059, Sec. 30(e)]

**Food service operations (TESDA Food and Beverage Services NC II)**
- Serving styles: **American (plate) service**, **Russian (platter) service**, **buffet** service. [SRC-0077, "Provide food and beverage services", range of variables 1]
- Sequence of service: greet → seat → take beverage orders → present menu → take food orders → (wine) → serve food → crumb the table → coffee/tea → present the bill. [SRC-0077, "Provide food and beverage services", range of variables 2]
- The **"3-minute check"** confirms guest satisfaction soon after serving (replenish condiments, add items, clear unused items). [SRC-0077, "Provide food and beverage services", element 2.5 and range of variables 3]
- Closing down: remove soiled dishes, handle **food scraps per hygiene rules**, clean and store equipment, reset tables, **turn off electrical equipment**. [SRC-0077, "Provide food and beverage services", element 6]
- Preparing the dining area includes stocking service stations, cleaning and placing tableware, filling water pitchers, and **wiping the necks and tops of condiment bottles**. [SRC-0077, "Prepare the dining room/restaurant area", element 2]

**Food Safety Act (RA 10611)**
- **Food business operators** must ensure food meets food-law requirements and that controls prevent, eliminate or reduce risks to consumers. [SRC-0058, Sec. 13]
- If an operator believes a food is unsafe, it must **immediately withdraw it** and inform authorities; if it may have reached consumers, inform them and **recall** it. [SRC-0058, Sec. 14(b), (d)]
- **LGUs** are responsible for food safety in food businesses such as wet markets, restaurants, catering, water refilling stations and **school canteens**; DA covers primary production; DOH covers processed and prepackaged foods. [SRC-0058, Sec. 15]

**School feeding (RA 11037, Masustansyang Pagkain para sa Batang Pilipino Act, 2018)**
- **DepEd** runs the **school-based feeding program** for undernourished public school children from **kindergarten to Grade 6**; **DSWD** runs supplemental feeding in day care centers for children **3 to 5**. [SRC-0062, Secs. 3(d), 4(a)–(b)]
- Each gives **at least one fortified meal** for **not less than 120 days** a year. [SRC-0062, Sec. 4(a)–(b)]
- A **fortified meal** supplies at least **one-third of the daily requirement** based on the PDRI. **Cycle menus** are standardized by the NNC and FNRI and adapted to age, location and local or religious food preferences. [SRC-0062, Sec. 3(a)–(b)]
- Other components: milk feeding with locally produced fresh milk, micronutrient supplements and **iodized salt**, deworming and health exams, **Gulayan sa Paaralan** school gardens, **WASH** facilities, and nutrition education. [SRC-0062, Sec. 4(c)–(h)]

**HACCP and the danger zone (Stage 7 addition; Wikipedia, tertiary)**
- **HACCP** = **Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points**: a **systematic, preventive** approach to food safety covering **biological, chemical and physical** hazards in production. [SRC-0254, lead]
- By 1997 **seven HACCP principles** became the standard. [SRC-0254, Principles]
- The **danger zone** is the temperature range where food-borne bacteria grow readily, roughly **4–63 °C** depending on the agency; potentially hazardous food should not be stored in it. [SRC-0253, lead]

## 3. Common mistakes

- **Wash ≠ sanitize.** Soap and warm water clean; the 77 °C dip, chlorine, steam or hot air sanitizes.
- **Do not towel-dry** sanitized dishes; air-dry on racks.
- **Glasses go upside down**, not right side up "to keep them ready".
- **Perishables ≤ 7 °C.** Milk 5–7 °C, meat and fish colder (0–3 °C).
- **DepEd vs DSWD.** DepEd: K–6 school feeding. DSWD: day care, ages 3–5.
- **Who checks school canteens for food safety?** The LGU, under RA 10611.
- **"One-third"**: a fortified meal gives at least one-third of the daily requirement, not the full day.
- **HACCP is preventive**, not end-product testing only.

## 4. Memory aid

**Dishwashing "Scrape, Scrub, Sanitize, Stand to dry."**
**RA 11037 "1-120-⅓"**: one fortified meal, 120 days, one-third of daily needs.
**"HACCP: Hunt hazards, Control at Critical Points."**
