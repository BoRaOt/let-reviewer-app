RA 7836, the Philippine Teachers Professionalization Act of 1994, makes teaching a licensed profession. Nobody may practice as a professional teacher without a certificate of registration and a professional license from the PRC, unless the law allows a special or temporary permit. The law created the Board for Professional Teachers. It has five members appointed by the President and serving three-year terms, and no one may serve more than two consecutive terms. The Board fixes exam dates, issues and revokes certificates, and adopts a code of ethical standards. The LET must be given at least once a year. For secondary teachers it has three parts: general education, professional education and the field of specialization. Every new registrant takes a professional oath before practicing, and a name enters the roster of professional teachers on registration.
