Why assess at all? TESDA's trainer standard lists several purposes. Assessment can **certify** that someone is competent, help **recruit and select** trainees, monitor work performance, find **training needs**, audit skills, **recognize prior learning**, classify a person against industry standards, and give **feedback on progress** during training.

**Recognition of prior learning (RPL)** matters in TLE: a learner who already sews well from helping at a family shop can be assessed and credited without repeating the whole course.

Evidence may be **direct**, such as watching the learner perform, or **indirect**, such as a supervisor's third-party report or a portfolio of past work. Good practice combines methods.

When a trainer prepares assessment instruments, TESDA asks that they be checked for **validity, fairness, safety and cost-effectiveness**, and that the method match the learning outcome of the module.
Memory aid: "Certify, Select, Monitor, Diagnose, Audit, Recognize, Classify, Coach."
