Safety is not only the employer's job. Under RA 11058, Section 4(b), every worker must take part in complying with OSH standards. That means four duties:
- use the safeguards and safety devices provided;
- follow instructions meant to prevent accidents;
- observe the emergency steps;
- **report any hazard they discover to their supervisor**.

TESDA's "Practice career professionalism" unit adds the ethical side of HE work. Its required knowledge includes work values and ethics (codes of conduct and codes of ethics), company policies and standards, fundamental rights at work including **gender sensitivity**, and personal hygiene practices. A professional sets priorities, uses resources and equipment economically, seeks training, and keeps licenses and certifications current. For cooks, bakers and beauticians, that means a TESDA National Certificate. Critical occupations must be assessed and certified by TESDA.
Memory aid: "Use it, follow it, report it — and keep your certificate current."
Sources: SRC-0063 Secs. 4(b), 18; SRC-0075 Practice career professionalism
