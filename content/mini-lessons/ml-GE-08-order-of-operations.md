When an expression has several operations, follow a fixed order, often remembered as **GEMDAS** or PEMDAS.
1. **Grouping symbols** first: parentheses, brackets, fraction bars.
2. **Exponents** next.
3. **Multiplication and division** together, **from left to right**. They hold the same position, so do whichever comes first.
4. **Addition and subtraction** together, also **from left to right**.

The common error is treating "M before D" or "A before S" as a rule. Example: 12 ÷ 3 × 2. Working left to right, 12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2 = 8. Doing the multiplication first wrongly gives 2.

Another example: 20 − 4 + 6. Left to right, 20 − 4 = 16, then 16 + 6 = 22, not 10.

With grouping: 3 + 2 × (8 − 5)² = 3 + 2 × 9 = 3 + 18 = 21.
Memory aid: "GEMDAS, left to right twice."
