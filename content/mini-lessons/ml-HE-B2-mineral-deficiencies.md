Two mineral deficiencies appear again and again on the exam.
- **Iron:** too little iron reduces the oxygen-carrying capacity of red blood cells, which causes anemia, with tiredness and weakness.
- **Iodine:** the body needs iodine to make thyroid hormone, which regulates basal metabolism, growth and development. Without enough iodine the thyroid gland in the neck enlarges, a condition called goiter.

The Philippines fights iodine deficiency mainly through salt. The ASIN law, Republic Act 8172, makes salt iodization a priority health program and requires restaurants, food outlets and stores to make only iodized salt available to customers. Remember that iron and iodine are minerals, not vitamins. A question that calls iodine a vitamin has a flawed stem. The deficiency pairs are iron with anemia and iodine with goiter.
Memory aid: "Iron for the blood, iodine for the neck."
