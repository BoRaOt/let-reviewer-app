The Occupational Safety and Health Standards Act, Republic Act 11058 of 2018, puts duties on employers and gives rights to workers. Employers must provide a workplace free from hazardous conditions, give complete safety instructions or orientation, especially to new workers, explain the hazards and how to prevent them, and use only approved devices and equipment. Workers have four rights.
- **Right to know** all workplace hazards and to get training on chemical, electrical, mechanical and ergonomic safety.
- **Right to refuse** work, without reprisal, when the labor department determines an imminent danger exists and the employer has not corrected it.
- **Right to report** accidents, dangerous occurrences and hazards to the employer and the government.
- **Right to protective equipment**, provided free of charge whenever the work is hazardous.

Safety signs must be posted in a language everyone understands.
Memory aid: "Know, No, Tell, Gear" — know, refuse, report, protective equipment.
