Two laws help a teacher recognize abuse.
- **RA 7610** gives special protection to children, meaning persons below eighteen and older persons who cannot protect themselves because of a disability or condition. Child abuse includes physical and psychological abuse, neglect, cruelty, sexual abuse and emotional maltreatment. It also includes acts or words that debase or demean a child's dignity, and unreasonable deprivation of basic needs.
- **RA 9262**, the Anti-VAWC law, covers violence against a woman by her husband, former husband, or a man she has or had a sexual or dating relationship or a child with. It also covers violence against her child. The harm can be physical, sexual, psychological, or economic abuse, such as controlling or withholding financial support.

A learner's home situation may show signs of either. The teacher's role is to recognize the signs and report through proper school channels, not to investigate alone.
Memory aid: "Under 18 = RA 7610; mother and child at home = RA 9262."
Sources: SRC-0029 Sec. 3; SRC-0028 Sec. 3
