The **Treaty of Paris**, signed on **December 10, 1898** by Spain and the United States, ended the Spanish–American War. Spain gave up the Philippines, and the cession involved a payment of **US$20 million**. The treaty was negotiated between Spain and the United States.

Under American rule, **Act No. 74 (1901)** created a public school system and made **English** "the basis of all public school instruction", with American soldiers serving as the first teachers until trained teachers replaced them.

The **Tydings–McDuffie Act**, the Philippine Independence Act of **1934**, set a **ten-year transition** to independence and a process for drafting a **Commonwealth** constitution within two years, leading to the 1935 Constitution.

In 1937, **Executive Order No. 134** proclaimed a national language **based on Tagalog**.
