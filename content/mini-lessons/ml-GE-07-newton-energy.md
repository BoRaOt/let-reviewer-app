**Newton's first law (inertia):** an object at rest stays at rest, and a moving object keeps moving at constant velocity, unless a **net external force** acts on it. That is why passengers lurch forward when a jeepney brakes.

**Second law:** **F = ma**. A bigger net force gives more acceleration; a bigger mass gives less.

**Third law:** when one body pushes on another, the second pushes back with a force **equal in size and opposite in direction**. The two forces act on different bodies, so they do not cancel. A rocket pushes gas backward and the gas pushes the rocket forward.

**Conservation of energy:** the total energy in any process stays constant. Energy only changes form, such as chemical to kinetic, or moves between systems.

**Simple machines**, such as levers, pulleys, gears, wedges and screws, multiply force, usually at the cost of a longer distance. Mechanical advantage measures the gain.
