**Student teaching**, also called teaching practice, is a supervised instructional experience. It is usually the **culminating course** of a pre-service teacher education program and lasts about one school term, long enough to complete the college's required tasks.

The student teacher is placed in a cooperating school and supervised on two sides. The **cooperating teacher** is the experienced, licensed teacher in the host school whose class the student teacher shares and eventually takes over. The **college supervisor** comes from the teacher education institution, observes and evaluates, and serves as the **liaison** between the cooperating teacher and the college's student-teaching office.

Student teaching differs from a **practicum**, which in some systems is for teachers who already hold a license and want to add another specialization. Both are college-supervised, field-based experiences.

On the LET, "Who is the student teacher's immediate mentor in the school?" points to the cooperating teacher.
