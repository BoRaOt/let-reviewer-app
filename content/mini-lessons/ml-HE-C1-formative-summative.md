Three words are often confused. **Assessment** is the whole process of gathering information about learning, from projects, portfolios, performances, observations and tests, and judging progress. **Measurement** answers "how much?" by assigning a number, such as 28 out of 30. **Evaluation** is the judgment made from that information, about a learner, a method or one's own teaching.

**Assessment for learning** is mostly **formative**. It happens during instruction so teachers can change their teaching and learners can improve. It may be **informal**, like noticing errors while students work, or **formal**, like a planned skills checklist.

**Assessment of learning** is mostly **summative**. It comes after instruction to certify mastery, decide readiness for the next unit, and give grades.

The key test for "formative" is use: results feed back into teaching before the unit ends.
Memory aid: "Formative forms the learning; summative sums it up."
