Many Manlilikha ng Bayan awardees are weavers and makers of everyday objects. Pairing the person, the craft and the place is a common exam task.
- **Lang Dulay**, T'boli of Lake Sebu, South Cotabato (1998): **t'nalak** abaca-ikat weaving.
- **Salinta Monon**, Tagabawa-Bagobo of Davao del Sur (1998): abaca-ikat weaving.
- **Darhata Sawabi**, Tausug of Sulu (2004): **pis syabit** weaving.
- **Haja Amina Appi**, Sama of Tawi-Tawi (2004): master **mat** weaver.
- **Eduardo Mutuc**, Kapampangan of Apalit, Pampanga (2004): **pinukpuk** bronze and silver metalwork.
- **Magdalena Gamayo**, Ilocano of Pinili, Ilocos Norte (2012): **inabel** weaving.
- **Teofilo Garcia** of San Quintin, Abra (2012): **tabungaw** native headgear.
- **Ambalang Ausalin**, Yakan of Lamitan, Basilan (2016): **tennun** tapestry weaving.
- **Ginaw Bilog**, Hanunuo Mangyan of Mindoro (1993): Mangyan script and poetry on bamboo.
Memory aid: "Lang–t'nalak, Magdalena–abel, Ambalang–Yakan, Eduardo–metal, Haja–mats."
