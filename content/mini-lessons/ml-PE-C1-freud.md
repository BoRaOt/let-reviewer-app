Sigmund Freud proposed five psychosexual stages: oral (0–1, weaning), anal (1–3, toilet training), phallic (3–6), latency (6–12) and genital (12 and up). Freud's theory matters historically because later theorists built on it. Erik Erikson, for example, trained in psychoanalysis and turned Freud's ideas into a psychosocial theory covering the whole lifespan. Modern psychology, however, does not support most of Freud's specific claims, and psychologists dispute the psychosexual stages as an explanation of personality. For the exam, know the order of the five stages and that Freud's theory is psychosexual while Erikson's is psychosocial. Do not confuse the counts. Freud has five stages that end in adolescence. Erikson has eight that continue into old age.
