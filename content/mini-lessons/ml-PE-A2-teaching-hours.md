The Magna Carta for Public School Teachers (RA 4670) limits actual classroom teaching to six hours a day. The schedule should leave the teacher time to prepare lessons and check students' work. When the needs of the service demand it, a teacher may be asked to teach longer, but never more than eight hours a day. Those extra hours must be paid at the regular rate plus at least twenty-five percent of basic pay. A separate rule covers duties beyond normal teaching, such as co-curricular and out-of-school activities. If they are done after six hours of classroom teaching, the teacher earns additional pay of at least twenty-five percent. Exam traps often swap six and eight, or claim that eight hours is the normal load. Remember that the cap applies to actual classroom teaching time, not to all school-related work.
Memory aid: "6 is the rule, 8 is the roof, 25 is the reward."
