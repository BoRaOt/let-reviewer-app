The **Cybercrime Prevention Act (RA 10175)** lists three groups of offenses. Offenses against data and systems include **illegal access** (hacking), **illegal interception**, **data and system interference** (including spreading viruses), **misuse of devices** and **cyber-squatting**, which means grabbing a domain name in bad faith. **Computer-related** offenses are forgery, fraud and **identity theft**. **Content-related** offenses are cybersex, child pornography, unsolicited commercial messages and **online libel**. Any crime committed through ICT gets a penalty **one degree higher** than usual.

The **Intellectual Property Code (RA 8293)** protects computer programs as works from the moment they are created; no registration is needed. A lawful owner may make **one back-up copy**. Copyright usually lasts for the author's lifetime plus fifty years. Installing pirated software on school computers is infringement.
Memory aid: "Hack, Fake, Post — three cybercrime families; one degree higher online."
Sources: SRC-0046 Secs. 4, 6; SRC-0044 Secs. 172, 189, 213
