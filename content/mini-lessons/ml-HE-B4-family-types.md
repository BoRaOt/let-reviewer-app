Sociologists sort families in several ways.
- **By how you enter them:** the family of orientation is the one you are born into, and the family of procreation is the one formed through marriage.
- **By structure:** a nuclear household has parents and their children. An extended family household includes at least one parent and child plus other relatives, such as grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins. Single-parent, blended, foster and cohabiting families are other common forms.
- **By marriage form:** monogamy is marriage to one person at a time, and polygamy is marriage to more than one.
- **By function:** the functionalist view says families do vital work for their members and for society. They provide physical, emotional and social well-being, parents care for and socialize children, and adult children often later care for elderly parents.

Exam items usually describe a household and ask you to name its type.
Memory aid: "Orientation = born in; procreation = formed by marriage."
