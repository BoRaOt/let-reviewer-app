On **June 12, 1898**, General **Emilio Aguinaldo** proclaimed Philippine independence from Spain at his home in **Cavite el Viejo, now Kawit, Cavite**. The **Act of the Declaration of Independence** was written by **Ambrosio Rianzares Bautista**. It was first ratified at Bacoor on August 1, 1898, and officially ratified by the **Malolos Congress** on September 29, 1898.

The Congress, meeting in Malolos, Bulacan, drafted the **Malolos Constitution**, the basis of the **First Philippine Republic**. Its first article declares that all Filipinos form a nation whose state is the **Philippine Republic**. Article 4 describes a government that is popular, representative, alternative and responsible, with **three distinct powers: legislative, executive and judicial**.

Remember the places: independence was proclaimed in **Kawit**; the Congress and Constitution belong to **Malolos**.
Memory aid: "Proclaimed in Kawit, built in Malolos."
