RA 9293 (2004) amended RA 7836. Under the original law, a graduate of arts and sciences needed only ten units of professional education to take the LET. RA 9293 raised this to eighteen units. It also covered teachers of vocational and two-year technical courses: they need a bachelor's degree in their field plus at least eighteen units of professional education. A second change concerns teachers who stop practicing. A registered teacher who has not taught for five years must complete at least twelve units of education courses before returning to the classroom. So if a question describes a nursing or engineering graduate who earned education units to become eligible, the law at work is RA 9293, not the original RA 7836 and never RA 4670.
