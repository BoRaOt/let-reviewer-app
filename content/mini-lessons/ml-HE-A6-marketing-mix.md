The marketing mix is the set of four decisions a business makes about what it sells, often called the 4 Ps. Product is the good or service itself, such as a bottle of calamansi juice or a sewing service. Price is what the customer pays in exchange. Place is where the customer buys it: a school canteen, a market stall, an online shop or a delivery route. Promotion is how the business communicates with buyers to attract them, through advertising, social media posts, flyers, sales offers and other messages. A common exam trap swaps place and promotion. If the question is about where or through which channel the buyer gets the product, the answer is place. If it is about telling people about the product or persuading them, the answer is promotion. A good plan keeps all four consistent with the target customers.
Memory aid: "What, How much, Where, How they hear" = Product, Price, Place, Promotion.
