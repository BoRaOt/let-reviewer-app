Under RA 10533 (2013), kindergarten and Grades 1 to 3 were taught in the learners' **mother tongue**, following Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE), which moves "from the known to the unknown".

That changed in **2024**. **RA 12027** **discontinued** the use of the mother tongue as the medium of instruction from kindergarten to Grade 3. Instruction reverts to **Filipino and English**, as the Constitution provides, and **regional languages serve as auxiliary media** of instruction.

MTB-MLE has not vanished completely. It may still be used in **monolingual classes**, meaning classes whose learners share the same mother tongue, if requirements are met: an official KWF orthography, documented vocabulary, reading materials, a grammar book, and trained teachers. DepEd and KWF must develop a **language mapping** policy.

Many older reviewers still describe MTB-MLE as current. On the LET, answer with RA 12027.
Memory aid: "2024: Mother tongue out as medium; Filipino and English in; MTB only in monolingual classes."
