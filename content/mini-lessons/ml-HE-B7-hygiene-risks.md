TESDA's Cookery standard teaches workplace hygiene in two steps.
1. **Follow hygiene procedures:** handle food safely, wash hands regularly, store food correctly, wear clean clothing, avoid cross-contamination, handle linen and garbage properly, and clean and sanitize.
2. **Identify and prevent hygiene risks.** Typical risks are poor food handling, storage at the wrong temperature, food left uncovered, poor personal hygiene, vermin, airborne dust, cross-contamination from bad cleaning, and contaminated wastes such as blood. Fix the risks that fall within your own responsibility. **Report** the ones beyond your control to the right person.

Cleaning the kitchen uses chemicals and clean potable water, follows a cleaning schedule, and ends with sorting waste and disposing of chemicals safely. Clean utensils are stacked in their designated place.
Memory aid: "Follow, Find, Fix or Flag."
