The **Katipunan**, the secret society that launched the Revolution, is generally dated to **July 7, 1892**, the night after **José Rizal's arrest**. Its moral guide, the **Kartilya ng Katipunan**, was first written by **Emilio Jacinto**.

In late **August 1896**, Katipunan members led by **Andrés Bonifacio** began the **Philippine Revolution** somewhere around Caloocan. The "Cry" may refer to the first clash with the Guardia Civil, or to members **tearing up their cédulas**, the community tax certificates, as a sign of rejecting Spanish rule, with patriotic shouts.

Because eyewitness accounts differ, the **date and place are disputed**. From 1908 to 1963, the event was commemorated on **August 26 at Balintawak**. In **1963**, the government declared **August 23** at **Pugad Lawin**, now in Quezon City, the official date. Exam items may test either the official date or the existence of the controversy.
Memory aid: "1892 founded, 1896 cédulas torn; official Aug 23 Pugad Lawin, older Aug 26 Balintawak."
