**Jerome Bruner** proposed the **spiral curriculum**: each subject or skill is revisited at intervals, at a **more sophisticated level** each time. Learners first meet the basic ideas, then return to them with more detail, and each return builds on and reinforces what came before. Harden and Stamper add that later encounters should be linked to earlier ones and should increase the learner's competence.

The spiral is not simple repetition. A Grade 7 learner might study basic food groups, a Grade 8 learner the nutrients behind them, and a Grade 10 learner planning balanced meals for a family budget. The topic is the same; the depth grows.

In the Philippines, this is a legal requirement. RA 10533 says the K to 12 curriculum shall use the **spiral progression approach** to ensure mastery of knowledge and skills after each level.
Memory aid: "Same topic, higher turn — like climbing a spiral staircase."
Sources: SRC-0180 lead; SRC-0021 Sec. 5(g)
