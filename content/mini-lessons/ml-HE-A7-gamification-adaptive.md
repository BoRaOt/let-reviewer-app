**Gamification** borrows game elements to motivate learners in ordinary lessons. Common elements are **points, badges and leaderboards**, stories and characters, choices, **immediate feedback**, challenges that grow step by step, leveling up and teamwork. A Kahoot-style quiz or badges on a weekly test are examples. Rewards alone can raise participation without raising learning, so the game elements must serve the lesson goal.

**Game-based learning** is different. Students learn by playing or building a game, and the learning sits inside the game itself. **Serious games** are designed with learning goals built into the play.

**Adaptive learning** uses computer algorithms, often with artificial intelligence, to give each learner customized materials and activities. A learner who answers well moves ahead; one who struggles gets more practice. It grew out of intelligent tutoring research from the 1970s and helps teachers reach diverse learners.
