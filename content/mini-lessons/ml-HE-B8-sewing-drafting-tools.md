TESDA's Dressmaking standard sorts sewing tools by job.
- **Measuring:** tape measure and meter stick, in either the English or the metric system. Pattern drafting adds the hip curve rule and the L-square.
- **Drafting:** tailor's chalk, pattern paper, a ruler with grid, a 45°–90° triangle, a hip curve, a French curve and a pencil.
- **Cutting:** scissors. Patterns are cut either with or without seam allowance.
- **Pressing:** flat iron, ironing board, sleeve board, press mitt and tailor's ham.
- **Machines:** single-needle and double-needle lockstitch machines, edging machines and buttonhole machines.

On the exam, first decide which stage the task belongs to: measuring, drafting, cutting, sewing or pressing. Then pick a tool from that group. A tailor's ham will never be the answer for drafting, and an L-square will never be the answer for pressing.
Memory aid: "Measure, Draft, Cut, Sew, Press — pick the tool from the right box."
