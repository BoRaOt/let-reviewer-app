The Code on Sanitation sets a clear routine for canteen dishes. First **scrape and pre-rinse** off food. Then **wash** thoroughly in warm water, about 49 °C, with soap or detergent, changing the water often if it is not running.

Washing removes dirt, but it does not kill enough germs, so the next step is **sanitizing**, called bactericidal treatment. Choose one: dip in clean water at **77 °C or hotter for at least 30 seconds**; dip for **at least one minute in a lukewarm 50 ppm chlorine** solution; use a steam cabinet; or use a hot-air cabinet at **82 °C for at least 20 minutes**.

Afterwards, let utensils **air-dry on wire racks** without drying cloths, which can re-contaminate them. Store them in a clean, dry place safe from pests, with **cups, bowls and glasses turned upside down**, and never on the lowest open shelves.
Memory aid: "Scrape, Scrub, Sanitize, Stand to dry."
Sources: SRC-0059 Secs. 24–27
