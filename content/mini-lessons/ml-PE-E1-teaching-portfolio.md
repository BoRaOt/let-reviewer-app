Field study and internship usually end with a **portfolio**. Seifert and Sutton define a portfolio as a meaningful, **purposeful** collection of work that tells the story of achievement or growth, not just a folder of everything.

They describe four pairs of choices, useful for designing a teaching portfolio.
- **For learning vs of learning:** reflection and self-chosen entries, or evidence to certify completion.
- **Progress vs current accomplishment:** drafts and early lesson plans that show growth, or only recent finished work.
- **Showcase vs documentation:** your best lessons and reflections, or every observation sheet and plan.
- **Working vs finished:** a day-to-day collection for developing ideas, or a polished version for an audience, such as a job interview.

A strong intern portfolio pairs artifacts, such as lesson plans, learner work and observation notes, with written **reflections** explaining what each shows.
Memory aid: "Purposeful, not everything: For/Of, Growth/Now, Best/All, Working/Finished."
