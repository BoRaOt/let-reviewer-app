Baking is precise, so TESDA's standard says ingredients are **selected, measured and weighed** by recipe, and the oven temperature is chosen for the product.

Key equipment: commercial mixers, scales and measures, bowls, cutting tools, ovens, moulds and cutters, baking sheets and pans of different sizes.

Know the dough families. **Lean-yeast dough** gives crisp- and soft-crusted breads, whole-wheat breads and sourdough. **Soft dough** gives loaves and buns. **Rich-yeast dough**, with more fat and sugar, gives sweet dough and laminated (rolled-in) dough. **Quick breads** such as muffins and shortcakes rise without yeast.

Techniques include beating, whisking, folding, rolling, laminating, creaming, kneading and proofing, then scaling, panning and baking. Finished goods are judged by color, texture, moisture, mouth feel and appearance, and stored with attention to temperature, light, air and labels.
Memory aid: "Weigh it, mix it, proof it, bake it, judge it, label it."
