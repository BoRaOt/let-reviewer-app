Suppose an HE teacher wants to know how much Grade 8 learners in a division spend on snacks each week. All Grade 8 learners in the division are the **population**. The 120 learners actually surveyed are the **sample**.

A number describing the population, such as the true average spending, is a **parameter**. The same number computed from the sample is a **statistic**. We use statistics to estimate parameters.

Data come in two kinds. **Qualitative** or categorical data describe attributes in words or labels, such as favorite snack or barangay. They are shown with pie charts and bar graphs. **Quantitative** data are numbers from counting or measuring, such as pesos spent or number of snacks. Quantitative data may be **discrete**, from counting, or **continuous**, from measuring.
