A **portfolio** is a meaningful, purposeful collection of student work that tells the story of achievement or growth. It is not a folder of everything.

Seifert and Sutton describe four pairs of purposes.
- **For learning vs of learning:** for-learning portfolios stress self-reflection and student choice; of-learning portfolios certify achievement, with less choice.
- **Progress vs current accomplishment:** progress portfolios show growth, sometimes with drafts; others show only recent finished work.
- **Showcase vs documentation:** a showcase holds the student's **best work**, chosen with feedback; a documentation portfolio includes **all** samples.
- **Finished vs working:** a finished portfolio is polished for an audience, such as a job application; a **working portfolio** collects day-to-day work and is **not meant for grading**.

In TLE, a cookery portfolio might include recipes, photos, self-assessments and teacher feedback.
