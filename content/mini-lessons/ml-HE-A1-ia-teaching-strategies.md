Exploratory Industrial Arts works best when learners build things and ideas themselves. **Constructivism** says learners actively construct knowledge from experience. In the **psychological** version (Piaget, Dewey), the learner is an independent investigator. In the **social** version (Vygotsky), learning is assisted by a more expert person.

**Inquiry learning** turns lecture on its head. The teacher poses a thoughtful question, such as "Why does this extension cord get hot?", and avoids giving the answer, guiding students to investigate instead.

**Cooperative learning** puts students in groups that are rewarded partly or wholly for group success. Jigsaw and STAD are common formats.

**Project-based learning** starts with a question or problem of interest. Students work together over an **extended period** and finish with a **product**, report or presentation, such as building and testing a simple circuit board or a small shelf.
Memory aid: "Ask (inquiry), Team (cooperative), Build over time (project)."
