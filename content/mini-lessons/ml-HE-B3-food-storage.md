Good storage keeps food safe and cuts waste. The Code on Sanitation separates two kinds.

**Dry storage** is for non-perishables such as rice, sugar, flour and canned goods. Use designated shelves, lockers and cupboards made of the same quality materials as food areas, and keep foods in **metal containers with tight covers**. A cool dry store, around 10 to 15 °C, is recommended.

**Cold storage** is for perishables. Keep them **at or below 7 °C** except while preparing or about to serve them. Each group has its range: **meat and fish 0 to 3 °C**, **milk and milk products 5 to 7 °C**, and **fruits and vegetables 7 to 10 °C** in cool rooms.

Refrigerators must be clean, in good repair, free from odors, fitted with a **thermometer**, and not overstocked, so cold air can circulate. A packed refrigerator cannot keep food cold.
Memory aid: "Dry and covered; cold at seven or below; meat coldest, produce coolest."
