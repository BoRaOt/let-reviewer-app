Competency assessment in TLE connects to national qualifications. The **Philippine Qualifications Framework**, set by **RA 10968 (2018)**, describes levels of educational qualifications and sets standards for their outcomes. Each level is described in terms of **knowledge, skills and values**, **application**, and **degree of independence**. The PQF harmonizes qualification levels across **basic education, technical-vocational education and higher education**, and supports lifelong learning and the **recognition of prior learning**.

Under the **TESDA Act (RA 7796)**, national occupational skills standards are set by TESDA-accredited **industry committees**. TESDA runs certification and accreditation, and accredited industry groups conduct trade tests. Each TESDA training regulation, such as Cookery NC II or Dressmaking NC II, states these standards unit by unit.

So a TLE teacher's classroom assessment should mirror the competency standards that the national assessment will use.
Memory aid: "PQF levels = Know, Apply, Independence; TESDA standards = Industry-set."
Sources: SRC-0042 Secs. 4, 5, 8; SRC-0043 Sec. 22
