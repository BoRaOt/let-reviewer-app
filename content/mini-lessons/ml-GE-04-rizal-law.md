**Republic Act No. 1425**, the **Rizal Law** of **1956**, is why every Filipino student studies Rizal.

**Section 1** requires courses on the **life, works and writings of José Rizal**, particularly his novels **Noli Me Tangere** and **El Filibusterismo**, in the curricula of **all schools, colleges and universities, public or private**. In college courses, the **original or unexpurgated editions**, or their English translations, must be used as basic texts. "Unexpurgated" means nothing has been cut out, including the parts critical of the friars.

**Section 2** obliges all schools, colleges and universities to keep **adequate copies** of the unexpurgated Noli and Fili, and of Rizal's other works and biography, in their libraries, and to include them in required reading lists.

So a college course must use complete texts, not abridged or censored ones, and libraries must stock them.
