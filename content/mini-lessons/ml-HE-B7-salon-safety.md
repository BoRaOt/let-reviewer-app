TESDA's Beauty Care standard has a whole unit on keeping the salon safe, clean and efficient. It has five parts.

First, **comply with health regulations**: follow the salon's personal hygiene rules and local health rules. Second, **check your own work area**: keep reception, stations and walkways clear and organized, dispose of waste properly, and remove spills, hair and other hazards from the floor right away. Linen is stored, cleaned and disinfected. Third, **check tools and equipment**: store them safely, prepare them for each service, and send faulty ones for repair. Fourth, **manage stock**: rotate stock, record levels, report shortages or wrong deliveries, and lift and carry safely. Fifth, **care for clients** so they feel comfortable and are consulted.

Many slips, cuts and infections in salons come from small lapses such as hair on the floor or unclean towels.
