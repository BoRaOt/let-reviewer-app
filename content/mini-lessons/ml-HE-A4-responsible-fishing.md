The Fisheries Code (RA 8550, amended by RA 10654) protects both fish and fishers. **Municipal waters** reach **15 kilometers** from the coastline and are reserved mainly for municipal fishers using boats of **3 gross tons or less**. A **closed season** bans catching certain species with certain gear in an area for a period.

Destructive methods are illegal: fishing with **explosives, poison or electricity**; using **nets with mesh finer** than allowed; **muro-ami** and any method that pounds coral reefs, with fines of up to two million pesos or more; and **converting mangroves** into fishponds. Catching mature milkfish breeders (sabalo) is also prohibited.

The FAO Code of Conduct for Responsible Fisheries adds the principle that **selective, environmentally safe gear** should be developed and given priority, to protect biodiversity and fish quality. In class, this links to food security: destroy the reef today, and there is no fish tomorrow.
Memory aid: "No boom, no poison, no shock, no fine mesh, no muro-ami, no mangrove loss."
