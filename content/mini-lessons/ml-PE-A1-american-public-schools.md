The Philippine public school system as a national, government-run system traces back to Act No. 74 of 1901, passed by the Philippine Commission during the American period. The act did several things.
- It created a Department of Public Instruction for the Philippine Islands, with its central office in Manila.
- It brought the schools set up under the military government under that department.
- It made all primary instruction in its schools free.
- It ordered that English be made the basis of all public school instruction as soon as practicable.
- It allowed soldiers to serve as teachers until trained teachers could replace them.

The act also funded a normal school, meaning a school for training teachers, and a trade school in Manila, and an agricultural school. When an exam asks which law established the public school system in the American period, or made English the medium, the answer is Act No. 74.
Memory aid: "Act 74 — free, public, English."
