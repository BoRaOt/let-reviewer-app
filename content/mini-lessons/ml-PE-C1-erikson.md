Erik Erikson updated Freud's ideas into a psychosocial theory with eight stages across the whole lifespan. Each stage is a crisis between the person and society.
- **Trust vs mistrust (0–1):** will my needs be met?
- **Autonomy vs shame and doubt (1–3):** can I do things myself?
- **Initiative vs guilt (3–6):** taking initiative in activities.
- **Industry vs inferiority (7–11):** am I competent?
- **Identity vs confusion (12–18):** who am I?
- **Intimacy vs isolation (19–29):** close relationships.
- **Generativity vs stagnation (30–64):** contributing to society and family.
- **Integrity vs despair (65 and over):** making sense of one's life.

A secondary teacher works mainly with learners in the identity stage. Activities that let students explore roles, values and future careers support that task.
Memory aid: "TAI-II-GI" — Trust, Autonomy, Initiative, Industry, Identity, Intimacy, Generativity, Integrity.
