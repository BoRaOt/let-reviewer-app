A safe, efficient shop, whether for carpentry, plumbing or cooking, follows **5S**, a method built on five Japanese words.
1. **Sort (seiri):** remove every item that is not needed in the work area.
2. **Set in order (seiton):** put each needed item in the best place for its use, so anyone can find it.
3. **Shine (seiso):** clean and inspect the area, tools and machines regularly; cleaning is also checking.
4. **Standardize (seiketsu):** make the first three a routine with clear rules and schedules.
5. **Sustain (shitsuke):** keep the discipline so the system does not slip back.

TESDA's plumbing standard lists 5S among the knowledge needed for tasks such as pipe leak testing, together with blueprint reading and the 3Rs of reduce, reuse and recycle. In class, a 5S checklist is an easy authentic assessment of workshop habits.
Memory aid: "Sort, Set, Shine, Standardize, Sustain."
