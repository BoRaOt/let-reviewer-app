After the pattern is ready, TESDA's standard follows a clear order. **Prepare the fabric**: check its quality, faults, width and selvedges, then soak or drip-dry and press it so it will not shrink after sewing. Know fabric properties such as weight, shrinkage, pile and grain. **Lay out and pin** the pattern along the grain line, mark seam allowances, and cut.

**Sew** the garment pieces, watching for puckering, uneven stitches, loose threads and skipped stitches. Sleeves may be puff, tailored or cup style.

**Finish** the garment: attach closures and accents such as zippers, buttons, bias tape, and hooks and eyes; fold, pin and sew hem allowances; then **press** with the right tools and heat. Before release, check for grease or oil spots, puckering, loose threads, missing buttons and uneven stitches. A garment is not finished until these faults are fixed.
