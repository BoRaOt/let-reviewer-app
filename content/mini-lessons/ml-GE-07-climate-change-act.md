The **Climate Change Act of 2009 (RA 9729)** created the **Climate Change Commission** to coordinate the country's response to climate change. The Commission is chaired by the **President of the Philippines** and has three commissioners appointed by the President. One of them serves as vice chairperson and heads the **Climate Change Office**, which supports the Commission. The Commission meets at least once every three months.

The law puts local governments in front. **LGUs are the frontline agencies** in planning and carrying out climate change action in their areas. Each prepares a **Local Climate Change Action Plan (LCCAP)**, consistent with the Local Government Code and the national framework.

For teachers, this links science to action: explaining the greenhouse effect is half the outcome; the other half is helping learners see how their barangay and LGU plan for floods, heat and typhoons.
Memory aid: "President chairs the Commission; LGUs make the LCCAP."
