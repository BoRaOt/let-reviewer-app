Software is the set of instructions that tells hardware what to do. It comes in two families.

**System software** keeps the computer itself running. The operating system (Windows, macOS, Android) manages memory and processes and gives you an interface. Drivers for printers and keyboards and **antivirus** programs also belong here.

**Application software** does a user's task. The core **productivity** applications are: **word processing** for memos, letters and reports; **spreadsheets** for numbers, formulas and charts; **presentations** for slideshows; and **databases** for storing and filtering large sets of records. Web browsers are applications too. **Specialized applications** serve one job, like accounting software, and **mobile apps** are built for phones and tablets.

Most programs share an interface: a menu bar or ribbon at the top, a toolbar of icons, dialog boxes that ask for input, and a status bar at the bottom.
Memory aid: "System runs the machine; Apps run your work."
