The Fisheries Code defines **aquaculture** as raising and culturing fish and other species in **fresh, brackish or marine** waters. The top Philippine products are **milkfish (bangus), tilapia, tiger shrimp (sugpo), seaweed and mud crab**. Tilapia, carp and catfish are mostly freshwater; shrimp and crab brackish; seaweed and green mussels marine. Milkfish is flexible and grows in all three.

The main systems differ by their floor. **Fishponds**, especially brackish ponds, are the traditional method. **Fish pens** enclose an area and use the natural lake or sea bottom. **Fish cages** have their own net bottom, cost less to build, but need more feeding. Mussels and oysters grow on bamboo stakes, and seaweed on stakes, lines or rafts.

In any system the farmer must manage water quality, especially oxygen, plus feed and temperature, and must not clear mangroves for ponds.
Memory aid: "Pen uses the floor; cage brings its own."
