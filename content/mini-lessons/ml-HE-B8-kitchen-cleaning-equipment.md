TESDA's Cookery standard splits kitchen cleaning into three jobs.

1. **Clean, sanitize and store equipment.** Choose the right chemicals and use clean, potable water on utensils, pots, chopping boards and working surfaces. Follow the manufacturer's instructions, then store or stack clean items safely in their designated place. Cleaning equipment itself is assembled, used and stored safely.
2. **Clean and sanitize premises.** Follow the cleaning schedule. Clean walls, floors, shelves, ovens and cold storage without harming people or property. If someone is hurt, follow first aid procedures.
3. **Dispose of waste.** Sort waste according to sanitary rules, and dispose of used cleaning chemicals by standard procedure, never casually.

Supplies include chemical dispensers, paper towels, cleaning agents and sanitizers. Workers should also know eco-friendly cleaning products and how cross-contamination happens.
Memory aid: "Equipment, Premises, Waste — clean, sanitize, store, sort."
