RA 11058 makes safety an organized system, not a poster on the wall. Every covered workplace needs a written **safety and health program**, made with the workers and submitted to DOLE. It covers things like a drug-free workplace, disease prevention, PPE, signage, toolbox meetings, accident reporting and an emergency plan.

An **OSH committee** watches over the program. The **employer chairs** it, the **safety officer serves as secretary**, and health personnel and **workers' representatives** sit as members. The safety officer, trained by DOLE, monitors and inspects, helps government inspectors, and can **issue work stoppage orders**.

All workers take an **eight-hour safety seminar**. The employer pays for the program as part of operating costs. Willful violations can cost up to **one hundred thousand pesos a day** until fixed, and everyone who manages the work is jointly liable.
Memory aid: "Boss chairs, Officer writes, Workers sit — and the Officer can stop the work."
Sources: SRC-0063 Secs. 12–16, 21, 28
