Animal production in exploratory AFA starts with knowing the animals and their purpose. The **carabao** is the Philippine water buffalo, a genetically distinct **swamp-type** buffalo. It is the traditional **draft animal** for plowing rice paddies and hauling goods, and it also gives milk, meat and hide. It is usually raised by smallholder farmers.

**Poultry farming** raises chickens, ducks, turkeys and geese for **meat or eggs**. Chickens kept for eggs are **layers**; chickens raised for meat are **broilers**. Layers may be kept in **battery cages**, in barns, or **free-range**, where birds roam outdoors by day and are housed at night for protection. Free-range systems allow natural behaviors like scratching and foraging, but crowded systems of any kind can lead to feather pecking and welfare problems. Most broilers are raised indoors.

Management covers housing, feeding, health and the purpose of production.
Memory aid: "Layers lay, broilers broil, carabao plows."
