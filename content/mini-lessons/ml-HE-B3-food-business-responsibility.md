A school canteen is a food business, so the **Food Safety Act (RA 10611)** applies. Its operators carry the **main responsibility** for safe food. They must know the food-safety rules that apply to their work and keep controls in place that prevent, remove or reduce risks to consumers.

If an operator believes a food it made, served or supplied is unsafe, it must **withdraw it immediately** and inform the authorities. If the food may already have reached consumers, it must tell them why and **recall** it. Operators must also allow inspections and cooperate with regulators.

The law splits government roles. The Department of Agriculture covers primary production and post-harvest. The Department of Health covers processed and prepackaged foods. **Local government units** cover food businesses such as wet markets, restaurants, caterers, water refilling stations and **school canteens**.
Memory aid: "Operator first, withdraw fast, recall if served; LGU checks the canteen."
