Good clothing care starts with the **care label**, which uses five basic pictures.
- A **tub** means washing. The number inside is the highest safe water temperature in degrees Celsius; American labels use dots instead.
- A **triangle** means bleaching. An empty triangle allows bleach, two slanted lines inside mean no chlorine bleach, and a crossed triangle means **no bleach at all**.
- A **square** means drying. A circle inside the square means tumble drying; dots show the heat.
- An **iron** means pressing. One dot is low heat (about 110 °C), two is medium (about 150 °C), three is high (about 200 °C).
- A **circle** alone means professional cleaning, such as dry cleaning.

An X over any symbol means "do not". So a crossed tub means hand-washing or dry cleaning only, and a crossed iron means do not iron. Reading labels before buying also helps you choose clothes you can maintain.
Memory aid: "Tub, Triangle, Square, Iron, Circle = Wash, Bleach, Dry, Press, Pro."
