A **network** is two or more computers or devices connected by cable or wirelessly so they can share data, printers and internet access.

Networks are named by size. A **personal area network (PAN)** links one person's devices in a small space, such as a phone and earbuds. A **local area network (LAN)** links computers in one building or campus, such as a school computer lab sharing a printer. A **wide area network (WAN)** spans large regions; the internet is the biggest one.

A **router** directs data from one network to another and lets several computers share one internet connection. Switches move data between connected devices, and gateways link a LAN to other networks.

On a network, a **client** is the device that asks for resources, like a student's laptop, and a **server** is the computer that shares them, like a file or print server.
Memory aid: "PAN = pocket, LAN = lab, WAN = world."
