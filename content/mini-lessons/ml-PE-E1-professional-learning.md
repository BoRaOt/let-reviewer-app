Reflection is personal, since only you lived your teaching experiences, but it grows faster with others. Seifert and Sutton group resources for professional learning into four kinds.
1. **Talking and collaborating with colleagues.** One-to-one conversations about teaching allow full participation and some confidentiality. **Group study**, where teachers meet to learn a new curriculum or plan school-wide activities, is especially good for beginning teachers learning from veterans.
2. **Professional associations**, which publish journals and hold meetings.
3. **Workshops and conferences**, which bring wider expertise than one school, though they cost time and money.
4. **Reading professional literature**, while asking what each study's purpose is and how it applies to your own class.

The conversations must be about educational matters to help. Teachers can also add to professional knowledge themselves through **action research**.
