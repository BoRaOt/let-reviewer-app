The Philippines honors artists through two different awards.

The **Order of National Artists** recognizes Filipinos who have made significant contributions to the development of Philippine arts. It was created by President Marcos's **Proclamation No. 1001 of April 27, 1972**, and is administered by the **Cultural Center of the Philippines** together with the **NCCA**. Because the proclamation is not retroactive, artists who died before 1972 cannot be named.

The **Gawad sa Manlilikha ng Bayan (GAMABA)**, set up by **RA 7355 in 1992**, honors **traditional folk artists** whose community art, such as weaving, carving or epic chanting, has been practiced for at least fifty years and is passed on to others.

So a painter like Amorsolo is a National Artist, while a t'nalak weaver like Lang Dulay is a Manlilikha ng Bayan.
