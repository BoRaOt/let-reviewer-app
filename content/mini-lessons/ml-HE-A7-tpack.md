**TPACK** explains what a teacher must know to use technology well. It starts with three kinds of knowledge: **content knowledge** (the subject, such as nutrition), **pedagogical knowledge** (how to teach), and **technological knowledge** (how tools work). Where two overlap you get pedagogical content knowledge, technological content knowledge and technological pedagogical knowledge. Where all three meet is TPACK. The model, by Mishra and Koehler, builds on Shulman's idea of pedagogical content knowledge.

So a good tech lesson is not the one with the most gadgets. It is the one where the tool fits both the topic and the teaching method.

Seifert and Sutton add that more technology lets the teacher move from "full-frontal teaching" to a "guide on the side". But it costs money, some activities do not suit it, and students need help separating trustworthy websites from fluff.
Memory aid: "TPACK = Tech + Teach + Topic, all at once."
