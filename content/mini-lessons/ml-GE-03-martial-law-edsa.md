**Proclamation No. 1081** placed the Philippines under **martial law**. President **Ferdinand Marcos** announced it to the public on **September 23, 1972**. It began a **14-year** period of authoritarian rule.

During this period the **1973 Constitution** created a parliamentary-style system with a **National Assembly** and a **Prime Minister**.

Martial law was formally lifted on **January 17, 1981**, by Proclamation No. 2045, weeks before Pope John Paul II's visit. But lifting it on paper did not end Marcos's hold on power; he kept broad powers for five more years.

That ended with the **People Power Revolution** on **EDSA**, **February 22 to 25, 1986**, a nonviolent revolution that led to Marcos's departure into exile and Corazon Aquino's presidency. The **1987 Constitution** followed, restoring a democratic and republican State.

A common trap: "martial law ended in 1981" is true only formally; authoritarian rule ended in 1986.
Memory aid: "Sept '72 declared, Jan '81 lifted on paper, Feb '86 ended at EDSA."
