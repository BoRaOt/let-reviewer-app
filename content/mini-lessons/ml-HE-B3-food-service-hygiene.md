Food that was cooked safely can still be contaminated at the serving counter. The Code on Sanitation lists simple rules for service.

Avoid **hand contact** with food and drink. Never use fingers to serve butter, ice or similar items; use tongs, scoops or spoons. Serve sugar in covered dispensers or single-serve packets.

Do not touch the parts of glasses, cups, plates and spoons that will touch food or a person's mouth. Hold glasses near the base and spoons by the handle. Keep disposable cups and plates in sanitary cartons and take them out without touching the food-contact surface.

Store clean cloths, napkins and towels in their own clean place, and put **soiled linens** in a **closed, labeled bin**. Keep ice-cream scoops in running water or very hot water, or wash and dry them after each use.
Memory aid: "Tools, not fingers; handles, not rims; clean cloths here, dirty cloths in the bin."
