A simple garment follows the same steps TESDA lists for dressmakers. First **prepare the fabric**: check its quality, faults, width and selvedges, then soak or drip-dry and press it so it will not shrink later. Next **lay out the pattern along the grain line**, pin it, and mark seam allowances before cutting.

While sewing, watch for the four common machine faults: **puckering, uneven stitches, loose threads and skipped stitches**. Each usually points to tension, needle or threading problems.

Finish with closures, hems, accents and final stitches, then **press**. Use the right heat for the fiber. TESDA's guide gives Level 1 for silk, Level 2 for cotton and Level 3 for maong (denim). Pressing tools include the flat iron, ironing board, sleeve board, press mitt and tailor's ham. Standard care methods are dry cleaning, soaking and drying, machine washing and tumble-drying.
Memory aid: "Check, Grain, Sew, Finish, Press — silk 1, cotton 2, denim 3."
