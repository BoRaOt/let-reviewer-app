Article 154 of the Consumer Act requires a consumer education program in the curricula of all public and private schools from primary to secondary level, and a continuing program for out-of-school youth and adults. That is why Home Economics teaches consumer education. The program covers consumer rights, everyday consumer problems, and four responsibilities.
- **Critical awareness:** be alert and questioning about the use, price and quality of goods.
- **Assertiveness:** act to make sure you get a fair deal, knowing that a passive consumer will keep being exploited.
- **Social concern:** be aware of how your consumption affects other people, especially the disadvantaged.
- **Environmental awareness:** understand the environmental effects of your consumption and conserve natural resources for future generations.

Exam scenarios describe a shopper's behavior and ask which responsibility it shows.
Memory aid: "CASE" — Critical awareness, Assertiveness, Social concern, Environmental awareness.
