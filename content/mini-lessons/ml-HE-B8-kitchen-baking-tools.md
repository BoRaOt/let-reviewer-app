Cooking starts with **mise en place**. Clean and sanitize the tools, assemble the ingredients by quantity and quality, thaw frozen items properly, and wash raw produce in potable water. TESDA's meat-dish tools include knives, a meat mallet, meat hooks, larding needles, sharpening and honing tools, slicers, a meat thermometer, weighing scales and kitchen twine.

Cooking methods fall into three groups:
- **Dry heat:** roasting, grilling, broiling, baking, pan frying, deep-fat frying.
- **Moist heat:** boiling, steaming, poaching, simmering.
- **Combination:** braising and stewing.

A bakery adds commercial mixers, scales and measures, ovens, moulds and cutters, baking sheets and pans. Ingredients are weighed to the recipe, and the oven temperature suits the product. Baking techniques include creaming, folding, kneading, laminating and proofing.
Memory aid: "Dry bakes, Moist boils, Combo braises."
