Matter divides into **pure substances**, which have a constant composition, and **mixtures**, which vary and can be separated by physical means such as evaporation. Pure substances are **elements**, which cannot be broken down by chemical change, such as iron or oxygen, or **compounds**, made of two or more elements and breakable by chemical change. Mixtures are **homogeneous** when uniform throughout, like salt water, or **heterogeneous** when they vary, like halo-halo.

Besides solid, liquid and gas, there is **plasma**, a gas full of charged particles, found inside stars.

A **physical change** alters form without changing what the substance is, like melting ice. A **chemical change** creates new matter, like iron rusting or a match burning.

The **pH** scale shows acidity. At 25 °C, **7 is neutral**, below 7 is **acidic** and above 7 is **basic**. Vinegar is acidic; soap is basic.
Memory aid: "Pure = element or compound; mix = homo or hetero; 7 is neutral."
