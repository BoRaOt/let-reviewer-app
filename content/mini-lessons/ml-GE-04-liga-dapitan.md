After returning home, Rizal founded **La Liga Filipina** on **July 3, 1892**, in the house of **Doroteo Ongjunco on Ilaya Street, Tondo**. It grew out of the Propaganda Movement and La Solidaridad, aiming to unite Filipinos and support peaceful reforms, including raising funds for the paper.

Only days later, on **July 6, 1892**, Spanish authorities arrested Rizal for supposedly having seditious papers and **banished him to Dapitan**, in Mindanao. The very next night, **July 7, 1892**, the **Katipunan** was founded: the reformers' hopes gave way to revolution.

Rizal spent **four years** in Dapitan. There he met **Josephine Bracken**, who became his common-law wife. In **August 1896**, with Governor-General **Ramón Blanco's** permission, he left to serve as a doctor in Cuba, but after the Revolution broke out he was arrested at a stopover in **Barcelona** on October 6, 1896.
