Beyond workload and job security, RA 4670 lists several benefits.
- Teachers get a compulsory, free medical examination before they start teaching and at least once a year after that.
- Physical and nervous strain is recognized as a compensable occupational disease.
- After seven years of service, a teacher may take a study leave of up to one school year at no less than sixty percent of monthly salary.
- An indefinite sick leave is allowed when treatment will last more than a year.
- On retirement, the teacher receives a one-range salary raise that becomes the basis of retirement pay.
- No one may deduct from a teacher's salary unless the law allows it, although association dues and insurance premiums may be deducted with written consent.
- Teachers may form and join organizations freely, without prior permission. Punishing a teacher for membership is unlawful discrimination.
Memory aid: "7-1-60" — 7 years of service, 1 school year of leave, 60 percent pay.
