Article XIV of the 1987 Constitution sets the State's duties in education. Section 1 says the State shall protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels and make it accessible to all. Section 2 lists concrete duties:
- keep a complete, adequate and integrated education system relevant to people's needs;
- maintain free public education in the elementary and high school levels;
- make elementary education compulsory for all children of school age, without limiting parents' natural right to raise their children;
- offer scholarships, loans and other incentives, especially for the underprivileged;
- encourage non-formal, informal and indigenous learning systems.

Section 5 guarantees academic freedom in institutions of higher learning and orders that education receive the highest budgetary priority. A frequent trap: free education covers elementary and high school, but compulsory covers elementary only.
Memory aid: "Free for both, forced for the first" — free elementary and high school, compulsory elementary.
Sources: SRC-0007 Art. XIV Secs. 1, 2, 5
