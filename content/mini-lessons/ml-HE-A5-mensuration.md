Mensuration means measuring, and every TLE trade depends on it. TESDA's unit on mensuration and calculations has two steps.
1. **Select the measuring instrument.** Identify the object and its regular geometric shape, choose a tool suited to the object and the job, and get the correct specifications. An alternative tool may be used if it does not sacrifice cost or quality.
2. **Carry out measurements and calculations.** Measure accurately, then compute with the four basic operations of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. Use fractions, percentages and mixed numbers where the task needs them, and check your own computation for accuracy.

The underlying knowledge is linear and geometrical measurement and trade mathematics: unit conversion, ratio and proportion, and formulas for the area, perimeter and volume of plane and solid figures. In Home Economics this appears in recipe scaling, fabric yardage and room measurements.
Memory aid: "Pick the tool, measure true, compute, then check."
