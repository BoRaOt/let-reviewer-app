# GE-07 — Science, Technology and Society

GenEd unit, **15 items** (10%). Outcomes: use scientific knowledge to **explain natural phenomena** and **protect Earth's resources**; show how **technology has benefited humankind**.

## 1. Plain explanation

**Life.** All living things are made of cells (cell theory). Plants capture sunlight in **chloroplasts** by **photosynthesis**, turning carbon dioxide and water into sugar and releasing oxygen. **Mendel** showed traits pass on in **dominant and recessive** patterns. Living things are classified **Domain → Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species**.

**Matter.** Matter is pure (elements, compounds) or mixed (homogeneous, heterogeneous). Physical changes keep identity; chemical changes make new substances. **pH < 7 acidic, 7 neutral, > 7 basic** (at 25 °C).

**Force and energy.** Newton's three laws (inertia; F = ma; action–reaction). Energy is conserved — it changes form but the total stays the same. Simple machines multiply force at the cost of distance.

**Earth.** Crust, mantle, liquid outer core, solid inner core. Rocks are igneous, sedimentary or metamorphic. **Plate tectonics** moves crust; subduction zones make volcanoes and trenches. The atmosphere is 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen; the **ozone layer** in the stratosphere absorbs UV.

**Protecting Earth (PH law).** Climate Change Act (RA 9729), DRRM Act (RA 10121), Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (RA 9003).

## 2. Testable facts

**Biology (OpenStax Biology 2e)**
- **Unified cell theory** (Schleiden and Schwann, late 1830s): one or more cells make up all living things; the cell is the **basic unit of life**; **new cells arise from existing cells**. [SRC-0092, 4.1]
- **Photosynthesis** uses **solar energy, carbon dioxide and water** to make energy-storing **carbohydrates**; **oxygen** is released as a waste product. It takes place in **chloroplasts**; **chlorophyll** in the thylakoid membranes absorbs light. [SRC-0092, 8.1]
- **Gregor Mendel** (1822–1884), an Augustinian friar in Brno, showed with **pea plants** that traits pass from parents to offspring **independently** and in **dominant and recessive** patterns; published **1865**. [SRC-0092, 12.1]
- The **Linnaean** taxonomic system (Carl Linnaeus) is hierarchical; the full classification has **eight levels**: **domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species** (dog: Eukarya, Animalia, Chordata, Mammalia, Carnivora, Canidae, *Canis*, *lupus*). [SRC-0092, 20.1]
- In the **water cycle**, water enters the atmosphere by **evaporation** (and transpiration from plants), condenses, and returns as precipitation. [SRC-0092, 46.3]

**Chemistry (OpenStax Chemistry 2e)**
- Matter is **pure substances** (constant composition) or **mixtures** (variable composition, separable by **physical** changes). Pure substances are **elements** (cannot be broken down chemically, e.g. iron, oxygen) or **compounds** (two or more elements; can be broken down chemically). Mixtures are **homogeneous** (uniform) or **heterogeneous** (varies from point to point). [SRC-0093, 1.2]
- A **fourth state of matter, plasma**, is a gas with many electrically charged particles; it occurs naturally inside **stars**. [SRC-0093, 1.2]
- A **physical property** does not involve a change in composition (e.g. density); a **chemical property** is the ability to change into another substance (flammability, toxicity, reactivity). A **chemical change** always produces new kinds of matter (e.g. **rusting**, burning a match). [SRC-0093, 1.3]
- At **25 °C**, neutral solutions have **pH 7**; **acidic** solutions **pH < 7**; **basic** solutions **pH > 7**. [SRC-0093, 14.2]

**Physics (OpenStax College Physics 2e)**
- **First law (inertia):** a body at rest stays at rest, or in motion stays at **constant velocity**, unless acted on by a **net external force**. [SRC-0094, 4.2]
- **Second law: F_net = ma** (acceleration is proportional to net force and inversely to mass). [SRC-0094, 4.3]
- **Third law:** when one body exerts a force on a second, the second exerts a force **equal in magnitude and opposite in direction** on the first. [SRC-0094, 4.4]
- **Conservation of energy:** total energy is **constant** in any process; it may change form or transfer, but the total stays the same. [SRC-0094, 7.6]
- **Simple machines** (levers, gears, pulleys, wedges, screws) multiply a force, often at the expense of the **distance** through which it acts; their benefit is measured by **mechanical advantage**. [SRC-0094, 9.5]

**Earth science (OpenStax Astronomy 2e)**
- Earth's layers: **crust**, **mantle**, **outer core (liquid)**, **inner core (solid)**, known from **seismic** studies. **Oceanic crust** (about 6 km thick, **basalt**) covers 55% of the surface. [SRC-0095, 8.1–8.2]
- **Igneous rock** cools from a molten state (all volcanic rock is igneous; basalt and granite); **sedimentary rock** is made of fragments or shells deposited by wind or water and cemented; **metamorphic rock** is changed by heat and pressure. [SRC-0095, 8.2]
- **Plate tectonics:** slow motions in the mantle move large crustal plates, drifting continents and building mountains; plates separate at **rift zones** and one plate sinks under another at **subduction zones**. [SRC-0095, 8.2]
- Atmosphere at the surface: **78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 1% argon**, plus traces of water vapor and CO₂. The **troposphere** is the bottom ~10 km where weather occurs; **ozone (O₃)** near the top of the **stratosphere** absorbs harmful **ultraviolet** light; CFC regulation protected it. [SRC-0095, 8.3]

**Climate Change Act of 2009 (RA 9729)**
- Creates the **Climate Change Commission**, chaired by the **President of the Philippines**, with three commissioners (one as vice chair and executive director of the Climate Change Office). [SRC-0051, Secs. 4–5, 8]
- **LGUs are the frontline agencies** in formulating and implementing **Local Climate Change Action Plans (LCCAP)**. [SRC-0051, Sec. 14]

**Philippine DRRM Act of 2010 (RA 10121)**
- The **NDRRMC** (formerly NDCC) is chaired by the **Secretary of National Defense**; vice chairs: **DILG – disaster preparedness**, **DSWD – disaster response**, **DOST – disaster prevention and mitigation**, **NEDA – rehabilitation and recovery**. [SRC-0052, Sec. 5]
- The **Office of Civil Defense (OCD)** administers the national DRRM program and formulates the NDRRM Plan. [SRC-0052, Secs. 3(z), 8]
- **LDRRMF:** not less than **5%** of estimated LGU revenue; **30%** of it is the **Quick Response Fund**. [SRC-0052, Sec. 21]

**Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000 (RA 9003)**
- **Segregation at source:** separating waste materials at the point of origin to promote recycling and reuse and reduce volume. [SRC-0053, Sec. 3(jj)]
- **LGUs** implement the law; segregation and collection of **biodegradable, compostable and reusable** wastes are done at the **barangay** level. [SRC-0053, Sec. 10]
- Each LGU must **divert at least 25%** of solid waste through reuse, recycling and composting **within five years**. [SRC-0053, Sec. 20]
- A **Materials Recovery Facility (MRF)** must be established in **every barangay or cluster of barangays**. [SRC-0053, Sec. 32]
- **No open dumps** may be established or operated after the law took effect; open dumps were to become controlled dumps within three years, and **no controlled dumps** are allowed five years after effectivity. [SRC-0053, Sec. 37]

## 3. Common mistakes

- **Eight taxonomic levels, starting with Domain**.
- **Photosynthesis releases oxygen**; respiration uses it.
- **Melting and dissolving are physical; rusting and burning are chemical.**
- **Third-law pairs act on different bodies**, so they do not cancel.
- **Outer core is liquid**, inner core solid.
- **Ozone is in the stratosphere**, weather in the troposphere.
- **NDRRMC chair = DND Secretary**, not the President; the **Climate Change Commission chair = the President**.
- **MRF in every barangay**; 25% diversion in 5 years.
